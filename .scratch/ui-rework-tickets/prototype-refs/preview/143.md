## 5. Data, payloads and rules

### Existing edits, blank occurrences and concurrency

Use existing `event_chance`, `event_occurrence`, `event_tree`, owning Activity `detail`, acknowledgement/exception and persistent-decision edits. The existing ordered `eventOccurrenceSchema`/`eventTreeSchema` and candidate collections can hold blank occurrences (no `tableRoll`); parents precede children and IDs remain unique. Requiredness belongs to preparation/Resolution, not rejection of partial drafts. No endpoint/validator extension is needed for this layout.

Implement one pure calculation of the required occurrence positions from current rules facts, then a fill-only preparation operation at the controller/service boundary:

- Required positions are one chance-triggered normal root; each due automatic source's count; two roots per guaranteeing Activity choice; two children of the eligible first normal Roll Twice; and one replacement for an event that cannot occur unless kept with a Rules Exception. Re-evaluate after upstream Activity/outcome changes, not just on visiting Event.
- Reuse existing matching IDs in their saved order. For missing positions derive stable identities scoped to the open draft, owner choice/source, parent and ordinal, with deterministic collision handling. Do not allocate a fresh UUID on every render or remount, use display indices as identity, renumber existing events, or change IDs when a roll changes.
- Submit only when required positions are missing. Normal/automatic topology uses `event_tree`; candidate topology uses the existing owner `detail` with full choice fields retained. Editing an existing roll/detail uses `event_occurrence` and preserves its identity/origin. Ordinary keystrokes in an occurrence's die never create that occurrence or a sibling; required empty fields exist first. Transient unparsed text never changes topology. Parent/chance semantic changes can make pre-existing positions applicable or cause missing blank positions to be prepared.
- Topology preparation is an ordinary saved draft edit, not a hidden synchronous local assumption. While it is pending, show stable blank placeholders and ordinary save feedback; do not send a child edit before its occurrence has been accepted. A failed preparation retains the user's saved data and exposes retry; the phase remains incomplete until required occurrences exist.
- Two devices mounting or observing the same requirement must converge to one set of identities, not duplicate roots/children. Existing `event_tree` conflicts with the whole event tree; candidate `detail` conflicts on its candidates field; an occurrence edit targets the occurrence. On rejection, refresh the accepted observation, retain every other player's value, recompute only missing blanks, and retry that preparation intent with a fresh operation identity. Do not replay an obsolete whole tree, overwrite a remote roll with a blank, or claim unconditional last-writer-wins for rejected stale operations. Transport retries retain their original operation identity. Bound retries and surface ordinary recovery if the conflict persists.
- Existing edits are separate transactions. Do not claim chance + topology, candidate + selection, or moving support across events is one atomic batch. Serialize dependent edits through the existing persistence queue and expose their incomplete intermediate state. Any need for a new argument, result or batch API must return to human approval rather than widening this issue silently.

### Active branches and legacy compatibility

The first eligible **normal** Roll Twice produces exactly two blank children and uses the phase's single expansion. Children, automatic occurrences and Activity candidates with a Roll Twice result say “Roll Twice again: reroll and enter the new die” and reuse their own field. They never create a replacement merely for Roll Twice. “No edit is sent for a second Roll Twice” in the decision means no branch-creation edit; the roll entry and its eventual replacement still use the ordinary existing roll/occurrence edit. A reroll remains required until the replacement value is entered.

An event that cannot occur instead has a nested replacement with blank dice, unless an explicit event-eligibility Rules Exception retains it. Replacement chains for ineligible events remain supported. Resolution order, duplicate Twice dispatch, no-additional-effect clauses and independent duplicate events come from the shared rule engine. Do not conflate a second event of a type with a second Roll Twice result.

Changing a parent away from Roll Twice hides its children from active resolution/readiness; returning it restores their values and identity. Clearing a required die leaves its required occurrence blank. Leaving a chance branch inapplicable, switching candidate or applying forced calm retains recorded input while excluding inactive effects. Never erase a rejected candidate, its nested targets/rewards or its acknowledgements merely because it is muted.

Existing drafts may contain manually assembled branches, surplus roots, historical replacement nodes for repeated Roll Twice, legacy nested support or explicitly stored `eventType`/persistent flags. Opening must not rewrite or migrate these existing values; additive preparation of genuinely missing required blank positions is the separate operation described above. Preserve their data and IDs; render retained values through the same event-specific controls, with a disclosure when currently inactive. Keep root-count/shape inconsistencies visible and repairable through explicit clearing rather than a generic JSON editor. Clearing an extra legacy occurrence's inputs can remove that now-empty surplus position through the existing tree edit; clearing a currently required position retains its blank placeholder. Do not silently pick and apply one of several conflicting roots or discard nonempty branches to obtain readiness.

For old repeated-Roll-Twice replacement chains, show the effective recorded reroll in the parent's visual block while preserving the stored child identity and its complete facts until the player explicitly edits/clears it. Legacy tree interpretation must remain supported in live drafts and record readers; this compatibility does not permit new repeated-Roll-Twice replacement children. Old immutable Resolution Records always render their recorded outcomes/version and are neither rewritten nor re-resolved with today's engine.

### Chance, shared rolls and rules versions

Use the shipped selection calculation: projected notoriety plus uneventful carry based on current rank plus due queued chance modifiers, bounded at 10–95%. Apply the operating settlement reputation to the chance percentile only. Preserve the established strict comparison: below chance triggers, equal/above does not. Show actual raw roll, adjustment, total and threshold separately. Automatic events precede normal handling; forced calm suppresses normal/candidate resolution but does not erase Activity spending or automatic events. Uneventful carry is derived from actual completed outcomes, respecting first-week, automatic/storm, duplicate calm and forced-calm exclusions.

Inherit [Upkeep's exact contract](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/140): legacy `{ dice, sides, provenance, modifiers }` or new `{ diceTotal, diceCount, sides, provenance, modifiers }`, strict alternatives, shared normalization. Complete arrays display their sum without read-time rewrites; incomplete/count/sides-mismatched rolls remain incomplete. New edits write totals, keep bonuses separate, preserve provenance/modifiers and natural single-die behavior, and keep out-of-range whole values as advisory. Clearing does not fabricate zero. Cover every nested officer, target, reward/loss and reaction roll, not just d100 fields.

Extra table/check modifiers retain stable source identity, signed value and required reason. Name known contribution sources in the breakdown, not the people supplying them (for example, “Officers +2 · Overseer +3”), following the final approved shared copy. Do not expose raw IDs or count calculated rank/officer/queued/Helpful/Strategist/Overseer bonuses twice. Existing settlement/reputation table-modifier entries are not reactivated by the new UI. Required rolls/DCs/ranges derive from the current rules and event mode, not a hardcoded generic d20.

**Ruleset Version:** the already-shipped reputation correction remains Version 5. Blank allocation, layout and equivalent roll/tree representation alone do not change Weekly Resolution and need no bump. Enforcing the approved candidate Roll Twice restriction changes current selected-candidate behavior: allocate the **next unused Ruleset Version** for that semantic change, coordinate with prior Upkeep and later officer changes, and update client/server resolution together. Do not hardcode 6. Tests must distinguish representation equivalence from that explicit before/after difference. Earlier Resolution Records preserve their original versions and outcomes; no historical rewrite/backfill is included.
