# Characters & officers (#141): don't copy from the prototype

Prototype: `prototype-approved/characters-officers` (`c6f90fa`), `/prototype/characters-officers?variant=B`. The behaviours marked "observed" were reproduced in the running prototype on 2026-09-27.

- **Move to… creates duplicate character/role pairs (observed).** The holder menu offers every other role, including roles the holder already has. Moving Bren from Marshal to Commandant lists him twice, and the card then reads "+14 training … (Bren Ironhand 5 HD + Bren Ironhand 5 HD + Dalla Rook 4 HD)". React also logs a duplicate-key error. Prevent duplicate pairs and count each distinct commandant once (§1 "No duplicate character/role pair"; §5 Rules semantics "each distinct holder").
- **Save silently overwrites another player's change (observed).** Open *Correct officers*, trigger "another player makes Kess the Spymaster" from the state panel, then save. The stale draft wins and Spymaster goes back to Vacant. The reducer commits the whole draft roster (`people`, `officers`, `teams`). Officer mode must replace only `roster.officers`, and roster mode only membership and overrides plus cascades. Merge unrelated sections from the latest source, and send any roster-baseline change into the conflict flow (§5 Correction projection, races and reference repair).
- **Conflict flow and wording (observed).** The "another player saves first" toggle is a scripted flag, not a baseline comparison. Its message is "Another player changed the officers while you were editing: Kess is now the Spymaster. Their change is shown; check your edits and save again." It keeps the reason, and a second Save succeeds. Use "Another player changed this section" with theirs/yours and *Start again from their values*/*Cancel*, where restarting clears the reason (§5 Correction projection).
- **Roster-leave warning names nothing (observed).** Switching Sera off the roster only says "Sera of Phaendar leaves the roster and loses all officer roles." The mock reads managed teams from the draft, where they are already cleared. The warning must name the affected roles and teams, as in "Removing Sera clears the manager of Smugglers and Town Watch." (§1 Correct roster bullet; [#114 decision](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/114#issuecomment-5844673552)).
- **The table isn't alphabetical (observed).** Rows follow mock insertion order (Aria, Bren, Kess, Tobin, Sera, Grom, Old Mattock, …). The spec requires "one alphabetical character table" (§1 tablet bullet; CHAR-02 name-sorted).
- **Rules arithmetic lives in `mock.ts`.** This covers the `checkRole` ability pairs, "first holder counts" for Strategist/Overseer, `hitDice ?? level` sums that include archived holders, and the `limitOf` manager limit. The strategist preview "5 → 4" and "leaves Activity slot 5 without an action" are hard-coded strings, and the pending "(Activity, slot 3)" comes from mock state. Derive effects, ties, limits and allowance from the shared rules utilities and the open Weekly Draft (§1 "rather than prototype arithmetic"; §5 Rules semantics; §3 "Real … authoritative rules replace mocks").
- **The record dialog has no validation (observed).** It's an uncontrolled `FormData` form with `Number()`. Clearing Hit Dice and saving creates a "0 HD" record and closes the dialog, with no field errors. *Archive* fires immediately and drops unsaved field edits. Use react-hook-form/zod, `noValidate`, field-local messages, record number ≥1 and retained input on failure (§1 Add/Edit and Hit Dice bullets; §5 last paragraph; CHAR-03/04/07).
- **Don't revive the roster-owned-kind scenario.** Setting the state panel to "kind lives on the roster person" enables a per-row PC/NPC select in *Correct roster* (`setPersonKind`), and `join` carries a kind in its payload. Kind is written only in the record dialog, and the roster copy is a mirror resolved from the current record at save (§3 "do not revive … roster-owned-kind scenario"; §5 Compatibility item 3).
- **Stand-ins and missing breakpoints (observed).** `PageFrame` fakes the shell (Week 14 / Finished weeks / Militia tabs, "Saved/Not saved yet"). *Go to Activity* and *Militia → Teams* are inert buttons. The board is a fixed `grid-cols-3` table: at 390 px the page scrolls sideways by 343 px, the reason bar doesn't wrap, and desktop runs full width instead of centered. There are no loading, failed, empty or no-militia states. Integrate the real shell, the §7 routes (`/campaigns/<id>/week?phase=activity`, `/campaigns/<id>/militia`) and responsive-pages variant C (§1 phone/desktop and state bullets; §3).
- **Captions and hint placement.** The Assign sheet's subtext ("People on the militia roster. Adding a role keeps their current one.") is the kind of explanatory caption the approval removed. The Change Officer Role hint appears whenever any PC candidate exists, when it should appear on assigning a PC. The Teams column reads "Manages 1 of 1 teams" (§4 caption approval via [#113](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/113#issuecomment-5844235691); §1 "Assigning a PC includes the approved … hint").
