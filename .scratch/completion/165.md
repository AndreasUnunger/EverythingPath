**What shipped**
- **Broke the Code:** item cards from the militia after Activity. A missing item stays visible, and Twice asks for no item.
- **Cache Discovered:** each cache has its own Attempt it / Let it happen and Secrecy check. Twice covers every cache left, or says "no additional effect". Older event-level entries are handled, and retrieved or lost caches are shown with a reason.
- **Festival and Market Day:** town cards say why each town counts as operated. A town that isn't operated needs a Rules Exception, and Twice keeps the same town or reaches every operated town.
- **Found Fire:** rewards per PC, with the value entered in gp and stored exact to the copper, decimal weight, the alchemical and poison flags, and a Rules Exception beside each reward. Removing a reward clears its exception first.
- **Hidden Agenda:** each recalculated Activity check is listed, and every newly required Activity input links to its exact Action Slot.
