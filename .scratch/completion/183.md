Implemented. Merged in `3fd61d3`.

**What shipped**
- **Correct roster.** Joining uses the record's kind (#180). Leaving removes that person's officer assignments and clears their team managers in the same correction, with a warning before Save that names the affected roles and teams. The Hit Dice override handles blank, zero, explicit and malformed values, and follows #196's effective Hit Dice.
- **Officer assignment.** Assign, move and remove across the six roles with one reasoned save. **Move to…** can't double-count a role. Candidate contributions show, and the Strategist allowance preview includes the slot it would no longer cover. Pending Change Officer Role actions are warned about.
- **The correction lifecycle is shared with Militia corrections** (#175–#177): a conflict with another player's change to people, roles, managers or kind is refused, with "Start again from their values", while unrelated changes merge. Refusal, unknown acknowledgement and week changes are handled.
- **The Militia "People & officers" fallback is retired.** Each field maps to a new mode: membership → On roster, Hit Dice → the override field, roles → Assign / Move to… / Remove, kind → the record dialog, the manager cascade → Leave, and the reason → a reason field with quick picks.

**Checks on the integrated branch** (`implement/148-ui-rework`): typecheck and lint pass; the full suite passed 2,208 tests with `rules:check` at 0 errors.

**Browser gate:** the full isolated-preview nightly gate, run in parallel on 3 cohorts, passed **40/40** in run `everythingpath-e2e-YIg0cp`. That includes the rewritten `character-ledger` journey: Correct roster, a two-session officer conflict with "Start again from their values", and a reload.

**Known limits:**
- A character created **before Setup** and left off the roster has no facts in the militia snapshot, so it can't join through Correct roster until its record is saved again; the page says so. This is tracked as a follow-up. Production has no such character.
- The ⋯ menu, Assign panel and On-roster switch are hand-built, because the repo has no matching shadcn components.
