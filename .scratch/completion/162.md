**What shipped**
- Editors for Activate Black Market, Broker Market, Secure Cache and Special Order. Items and caches come from the militia's state at that slot, so an item taken from a cache earlier can be sold later.
- Purchases can be added, edited and removed, each with its own availability answer. Missing items, caches and settlements stay listed, with a Militia corrections link.
- Prices go through `gp-money.ts`, exact to the copper, and weights take decimals.
- **Gate fix:** the "Save availability of <item>" button couldn't wrap and overflowed a 390 px phone (up to 637 px with a long item name). It now wraps, keeping its touch height. The change is in the shared `ActivityText`, so all economy editors are covered, with a regression test.
