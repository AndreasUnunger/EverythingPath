**What shipped**
- Its own editor in the selected slot's details for each of the ten information, mission and event-influence actions. Options are computed at each slot's position, and missing references are kept.
- A Covert Action's recorded following choice is never retargeted by move, swap or clear. Two candidate sources keep independent sets and selections.

- Guarantee Event and Manipulate Events show a read-only candidate summary built from Event's own facts, with a "Roll and choose in Event" button.
- **This closes #191's browser gap:** the Guarantee Event step covers two-device candidate preparation, the in-place Roll Twice reroll, choosing a candidate and the Activity summary.
