Implemented. Merged in `76a7b23`, which integrates #178's retirement of the full correction form.

**What shipped**
- **The character record owns the kind.** Every record API stores only PC or NPC; old clients' `officer_npc` and `other_npc` are normalized on write. Every character write updates its roster mirror in the same revision, and a stale or old-client correction can't bring back an old kind.
- **Setup stores the record's kind.** The read-only record-owned Kind appears in guided Setup's People step and roster editor (and in the People & officers correction, since retired by #183).
- **The Setup browser envelope is version 2,** with a version 1 migration on read that keeps raw input, Hit Dice and attempt identity.
- **Relabelling changes no rule outcome.** Preview and Confirmation give the same results before and after, thanks to #196's role-aware rules. History keeps its recorded kinds.

**Decision recorded:** spec #141 §5.1 treats a record with no kind as a PC, and the record wins. A read-only production check (2026-09-28) found all 6 characters and roster entries are `pc`, and the user confirmed there will be no data changes before release. So this edge case can't occur, and #181's live-row migration was closed as not planned.

**Checks:** typecheck and lint pass, and the full suite passes with `rules:check` at 0 errors.

**Browser gate:** the `existing-militia` read-only Kind step, shown across a reload, has passed in the green gates since the merge, most recently `everythingpath-e2e-YIg0cp` (40/40).

**Legacy paths:** the leftover legacy-kind readers and the envelope v1 migration are recorded in `docs/legacy-compatibility-inventory.md` for later removal.
