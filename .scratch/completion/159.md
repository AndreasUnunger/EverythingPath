**What shipped**
- The shared Action Slot board and grouped picker: move and swap choices, Clear, and **Remove** for genuinely empty slots beyond the allowance. Removal uses the new `remove_slot` Weekly Draft edit, which the server validates: it rejects replayed, duplicate, occupied and in-allowance removals, closed drafts and other campaigns, and handles concurrent fill and remove.
- The team selector for every action, Operating from, check totals, moving Helpful (across two devices, with partial-failure recovery) and reasoned modifiers.
- Gate fixes:
  - Focus returns to the invoking slot when the picker closes (ACT-02), in `499d6d2`.
