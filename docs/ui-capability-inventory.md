## How to read this

- **Id**: stable. New items get new ids; retired items keep theirs and are marked as removed with who signed off.
- **Where**: the route, then the component file under `src/`. `components/` is abbreviated `c/`, and `c/weekly-draft-workspace/` is abbreviated `wdw/`.
- **E2E**: the scenario that exercises the capability through the browser. `—` means no browser scenario covers it today; the cell then names the unit, component or convex-test coverage. A rewritten suite must keep at least this coverage.
- **Gate runs** are cited by the suffix of their `everythingpath-e2e-…` id; their artifacts are under `e2e-artifacts/e2e-local-andreasununger-slot-0/`. `fX7jhJ` is the final nightly gate: **42/42 journeys on their first attempt** at `349ad0c`, parallel across three cohorts with the isolation check clean. `SK580N` (41/42 at `0573d0b`) and `cLnOsI` (41/42 at `e4c56af`) each failed only the Characters & officers phone/desktop correction check on Chromium tablet; the cause was a transient window resize during Chromium's full-page screenshot flipping the layout breakpoints, fixed in `349ad0c`. `YIg0cp` is the earlier full-green gate: 40/40 at `9589fe1`.

## Observations for the designs

Facts about today's UI that later tickets may want to decide on. None of them is a decision.

- Since #149 the week screen exists once, at `/campaigns/<id>/week`; every link stays within the campaign. Setup is reachable from the campaign home, the Week and Militia empty states and the bottom/top bar's four sections do not include it. The selected campaign is in the address on campaign pages; `/campaigns` itself still defaults to the first campaign.
- The Week frame (rollout 2) owns the stepper, reference panel, footer and phone status strip. The shell reserves the top bar's right side (before organization/account) for week-only status controls and the phone bottom bar's top edge for the status strip.
- Officer assignment and roster membership (LEDG-03) are corrected on Characters & officers (#183; the Militia People & officers fallback is retired); manager assignment stays in Militia **Correct teams**.
- Capabilities whose E2E cell starts with `—` have no browser scenario and name their unit, component or convex-test coverage instead. They include submitting a new campaign (the fixture harness cannot clean up UI-created campaigns), archiving characters, the maintenance pause, the sign-in and organization states and most error states. History navigation now has browser coverage (HIST-02–04).
