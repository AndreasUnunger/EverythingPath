# Characters navigation

Every Character Sheet uses `/characters/<characterId>`, independently of campaign membership. `characterSheetPath(characterId, origin?)` is the single sheet URL builder. Origins are navigation context, never authorization: `from` must name a recognized local app page. `readCharacterSheetOrigin` reads the address and `resolveNavigationLocation` recognizes its page; `resolveCharacterSheetBack` supplies the trusted destination and label. `OriginOrganization` explicitly distinguishes `{ kind: 'organization', id }`, `{ kind: 'personal' }` and `{ kind: 'unrecorded' }`. In the address, `organizationId` carries the source organization, a blank value represents the personal account, and an omitted value means no organization was recorded. Unknown or external origins fall back to the Characters area.

| Page                                             | Route                                |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Active organization's campaigns                  | `/campaigns`                         |
| Campaign Home                                    | `/campaigns/<campaignId>`            |
| Campaign Characters                              | `/campaigns/<campaignId>/characters` |
| Militia Characters & officers ledger             | `/campaigns/<campaignId>/officers`   |
| Owned accessible Characters across organizations | `/characters`                        |
| Independent creation                             | `/characters/new`                    |
| Character Sheet                                  | `/characters/<characterId>`          |
| Character Spells                                 | `/characters/<characterId>/spells`   |

`characterSpellsPath(characterId, origin?, spellcastingId?)` builds a sheet's Spells page URL with the sheet's `from` and `organizationId`; navigation treats the Spells page as part of its sheet, and its only Back is "Sheet". The old `/campaigns/<campaignId>/characters/<characterId>` entry redirects to the independent sheet with Characters & officers as its origin. `characterCreatePath` carries campaign and source context to the creation form. The form resolves its campaign through an authorized campaign list, and prepared campaign/personal creation opens a Full sheet. Personal lists offer creation only with the persisted `user.characterSheetDemo` grant; campaign lists offer it only when their authorized campaign has `e2eFixture`. Creation stays hidden while eligibility is loading or unavailable. The migration Write Gate and isolated-fixture eligibility remain enforced.

`buildAppNavigation` provides top-level or campaign section links, militia rail pages, fixed phone tabs and the origin's phone page strip. The phone tabs remain Campaign, Militia, Characters and More at every depth. Campaign opens the current campaign, then the last accessible campaign visited in the active organization, then the campaign list. Militia reopens the last militia page for that campaign, preserving page selectors. Remembered entries cannot expose a campaign missing from the authorized list or direct navigation to another campaign or host. An absent militia remains dimmed and offers an explanation and Switch campaign. `useAppNavigation` persists this context for the browser session across layout remounts.

`campaign.listNavigationContexts` supplies authorized campaign militia existence and optional open-week number without loading a full workspace. A militia can exist before its first week is initialized. `useCampaignShellNavigation` combines this information with authorized campaign records and distinguishes militia information still loading from an absent militia. While loading, navigation keeps the militia destinations available instead of showing the absent-militia explanation. Without a militia, the phone page strip offers Home and Characters. A sheet hides the militia rail while retaining its origin's selected section and phone strip; a Characters-area origin has no strip.

`character.listOwned` returns No campaign first, then accessible owned Characters grouped by campaign and organization. Ownership alone does not grant campaign access. `character.listCampaignCharacters` uses the shared accessible-Characters reader and includes archived records, owner names and roster membership; a missing owner has a null name. Character lists reject more than 4096 records instead of returning an incomplete list. Organization group names come from paginated Clerk memberships. The place picker follows the active organization even while a sheet retains a campaign origin from another organization.

Campaign list rows with a prepared sheet (`character.sheetMode`) use `characterSheetPath` with the campaign Characters origin. Legacy rows instead open Characters & officers when `campaign.listNavigationContexts` reports a militia, including before its first week is initialized. Without a militia, or while militia existence is loading, legacy names have no link. These destinations preserve legacy militia authority until the explicit sheet cutover.

`useCharacterSheetNavigation` reads the sheet by Character identity, takes its current campaign from the authorized response, and activates that campaign's organization once on opening. A manual organization change preserves the independent URL and origin. Back uses the existing departure guard, waits for pending automatic activation, restores the recorded source organization or personal account, and then opens the origin. Failed automatic changes retain the sheet and offer retry; a failed Back activation leaves navigation to the guard's retry handling. Asynchronous results remain scoped to the sheet that started them.

`characterMoves.destinations` provides authorized movement eligibility, current ownership, the current campaign and accessible prepared destination campaigns. `useCharacterMove` reads this projection by Character identity: only the current owner of an eligible prepared Character can join, leave or move it. Its picker excludes the current campaign. Joining from no campaign and moving from another campaign keep the same independent sheet URL and never add militia roster or officer assignments. Leaving targets no campaign; every departure makes a Militia-only Character Full and removes its old roster, officer and manager assignments when the complete move is published. The controller keeps saving, refusal and uncertain-outcome feedback beside the movement control, shows bounded preservation progress and resumes an existing move after reopening. A lost reply requires checking the recorded operation before retrying; completed operations are acknowledged without another write, and replies from a previously viewed Character are ignored. The presentation contract also covers Add from my characters on the campaign Characters page, identifying Characters that move from another campaign.

Behavior is covered at the public route/model/controller seams by `src/lib/campaign-routes.test.ts`, `src/lib/app-navigation.test.ts`, shell hook tests, Character navigation hook tests, sheet navigation hook tests, shell/route presentation tests and `convex/characterNavigation.integration.test.ts`. Cross-organization behavior is covered by Convex and hook tests. The registered `characterNavigation` browser journey checks owned groups within one organization, sheet origins and fixed shell navigation with isolated fixtures. It must run outside the implementation sandbox.

Leaving first opens a confirmation matching the approved `LeaveCampaignDialog`: it names current roster, officer and team-manager assignments that will be removed, preserves campaign homebrew as Character copies, and explains that only the owner can see the resulting private Character. The ready departure repeats these consequences beside Complete move. Add from my characters can resume only a move targeting that dialog’s campaign; a pending move elsewhere appears disabled as Moving to the destination campaign. Arrival suggests adding a militia roster place only when that campaign has a militia. Technical movement refusals are translated into player-facing messages; diagnostic details remain in structured console errors.

Prepared moves discover Spell lists in bounded pages, then check their current membership during atomic publication. Source, global and destination freshness scans read Spell definitions only; unrelated non-Spell catalog entries do not prevent a small move. Each scan still includes Spells on unrelated lists and is limited to 8,192 rows plus the remaining transaction read budget. Exceeding either bound leaves the Character in its current campaign; moving through larger Spell catalogs needs an exact membership index or a paged freshness protocol before broader activation.
