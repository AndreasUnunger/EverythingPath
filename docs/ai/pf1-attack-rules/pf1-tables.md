## Table: Routine Option Scaling by BAB (derived)

Power Attack: "–1 penalty ... +2 bonus ... When your base attack bonus reaches +4, and every 4 points thereafter, the penalty increases by –1 and the bonus to damage increases by +2"; "+50%" two-handed, one-handed in two hands, or a primary natural weapon with 1-1/2 Strength; "–50%" off hand or secondary natural. Deadly Aim: same steps, ranged, no hand multiplier. Combat Expertise: "–1 ... +1 dodge ... every +4 thereafter, the penalty increases by –1 and the dodge bonus increases by +1". Monks use the flurry BAB while flurrying (FAQ `v5748eaic9o72`).

| BAB | Penalty (PA, DA, CE) | Power Attack ×1 | PA ×1.5 | PA ×0.5 | Deadly Aim | Combat Expertise dodge |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| +1 to +3 | –1 | +2 | +3 | +1 | +2 | +1 |
| +4 to +7 | –2 | +4 | +6 | +2 | +4 | +2 |
| +8 to +11 | –3 | +6 | +9 | +3 | +6 | +3 |
| +12 to +15 | –4 | +8 | +12 | +4 | +8 | +4 |
| +16 to +19 | –5 | +10 | +15 | +5 | +10 | +5 |
| +20 | –6 | +12 | +18 | +6 | +12 | +6 |

Power Attack and Deadly Aim require BAB +1. Combat Expertise has no BAB prerequisite (Int 13 only), so its first row also covers BAB +0.

## Table 8–7: Two-Weapon Fighting Penalties (CRB p. 202, printed)

| Circumstances | Primary Hand | Off Hand |
|---|---:|---:|
| Normal penalties | –6 | –10 |
| Off-hand weapon is light | –4 | –8 |
| Two-Weapon Fighting feat | –4 | –4 |

"Light" includes unarmed strikes, the off-hand end of a double weapon, light shields, armor spikes, darts and shuriken thrown; heavy shields, bolas, javelins, nets and slings count as one-handed (CRB pp. 149, 202). Hand and light crossbows fired in each hand: "as if attacking with two light weapons"; heavy: "two one-handed weapons", plus the one-handed firing penalty (–2 light, –4 heavy).

Any weapon use in the same full attack makes all natural attacks secondary.
