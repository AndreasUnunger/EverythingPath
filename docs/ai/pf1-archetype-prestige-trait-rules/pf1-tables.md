## Table: Staggered Advancement, Medium XP Track (PU p. 42, Table 1–8)

At the 25%, 50% and 75% tiers the character takes one of: BAB, 50% of the level's hp, or saves, each once per level. Half the level's skill ranks (rounded down) come at the 50% tier. At the full level come the remaining hp and skill ranks and all class features. Feats, ability increases and spells follow the normal level.

|---|---|---|
| Healthy | above 3/4 of maximum | — |

## Table: Favored Class Benefit per Level (CRB p. 31; APG p. 8)

| Choice | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| +1 hit point | CRB | — |
| +1 skill rank | CRB | — |

The choice is fixed per level (CRB). Players may alternate between choices from level to level (APG). Half-elves have two favored classes unless they take Arcane Training.
