## Family rulings (first batch of the family; for review)

This is the family's first batch, so these choices are proposed as the family's precedent. They follow the gear precedent of #424 (armors-and-shields batch 1) wherever it applies.

1. **Every weapon action requires PZO1131, so no weapon can be admitted yet.** Foundry v11 records `activation.unchained: {type: attack}` on every weapon attack action. The settled ruling (curation.md, "Unchained action costs") requires the held PZO1131 for Unchained action-cost metadata anywhere in a record's bound closure, so all 73 records with an action require it for their own action, and Nagant M1895 Revolver, Repeating Hand Crossbow and Light Crank Crossbow also through a bound linked body (the revolver, the repeating heavy crossbow, the heavy crank and light crossbows). The 23 ammunition and siege-ammunition records have no action. The ruling is applied as settled; the reviewer may want to weigh whether bare attack-type metadata on weapon actions should count, since it holds the whole weapon family until S-02/#736 resolves.
2. **Admission bar (as #424).** An item could be admitted when a PCGen Core Rulebook row is a complete direct comparison (its `DESC` is the pinned body and every statistic matches) and its required notices are all reviewed. Three records meet the content bar: Punching Dagger (`cr_equip_arms_armor.lst:143`), Trident (`:169`) and Arrow (`:11`) are the CRB `DESC` word for word with every compared statistic matching. They are held only on their notices: each cites Ultimate Equipment (PZO1123, held), and the two weapons need PZO1131. Their hold reasons say so, so they can be re-assessed when those holds lift. No ammunition record cites only the Core Rulebook.
3. **Books (as #424).** Every record requires its cited books, the books of the PCGen rows it follows (comparison and leads), its linked bodies' books and the books of rules its body inherits, plus PZO1110 for the core weapon rules. Rows whose statistics or wording the record doesn't follow are rejected and their books not required (12 rejected rows, such as Adventurer's Armory's 2 lb. shotel and the Elves of Golarion arrows with other wording). Codes without a reviewed Registry identity are held as `weapons-and-ammo-425-notice-<code>-complete-chain`: 27 holds registered in `held-notices.json`. PZO9276 (Belkzen, Hold of the Orc Hordes) and DYN0032-E (Worldscape #2) are required for the first time in any batch; neither has a Registry identity.
4. **Inherited rules.** Recorded in `inheritedRuleNotices` with the evidence that names the book: PZO1112 for the vampire weaknesses (Heartstake Bolt) and lycanthropes (Silver Firearm Bullet) from their pinned Bestiary template records; PZO1123 for marker dye (Dye Arrow) and the grapple quality (Barbed Bolt), both cited by page in the body; PZO1118 for the ballista (Weighted Bolt, Flechette Bolt, from the pinned ballista records) and the indirect-fire and advanced-firearms rules (Aasen Mortar, Thark Rifle, cited in the body); PZO1121 for the vishkanya's weapon familiarity (Sanpkhang, "ARG" in the body); DYN0032-E for the Thark Rifle's own source line. ## Importer and data gaps

Reported, not fixed (owner amendment 2: no importer code changes):

- **Imported weapon detail is not the sheet's weapon shape (#761; affects every weapon).** `mapWeapon` in `scripts/catalog/details.ts` writes `{ baseTypes, groups, proficiency, handedness, dice, damageTypes, threat, mult, finesse, thrown, rangeIncrement }`, with Foundry's `weaponSubtype` as handedness (`light`, `1h`, `2h`, `ranged`, and `assault`, `direct`, `other` for siege records) and `subType` as proficiency (also `siege` and `misc`). The stored sheet weapon (`convex/schema.ts`, catalog entry `detail.weapon`) requires `baseType` and accepts handedness `light`, `oneHanded` or `twoHanded` with a separate `attackType`, plus `thrownRangeIncrement`, `strengthDamage`, `reload` and `otherEnd`. Minimal reproduction: import Punching Dagger (`pf1/05NnBuNnkMsaNoXf`) and its `detail.weapon` has no `baseType` and `handedness: "light"`; Heavy Mace has `handedness: "1h"`, which the stored validator rejects. So no imported weapon could be released as a usable sheet weapon until a conversion lands. The batch tests use a test-only adapter (`tests/fixtures/weapons-and-ammo-425/sheet.ts`) for melee weapons to demonstrate the reviewed values.
- **Imported ammunition isn't ammunition to the sheet (#761; affects all 23 ammunition records).** The importer sets no `detail.ammunition`, which an Attack Routine needs before it can fire an item (`routineAmmunition` in `src/lib/character-sheet-attacks.ts`), so no routine can select any of this batch's ammunition. The three ammunition Modifiers (Pronged Arrow +1, Heartstake Bolt −2 on attack, Silver Firearm Bullet −1 on damage) are scoped to the attacks that fire the item (`weapon: $self`) and reach no attack until #761 lands. Each record's end state says so.
- **Damage formulas with a Strength term (own entry).** Shortbow's damage part is `sizeRoll(1, 6, @size) + min(@abilities.str.mod, 0)[Strength]`, so the importer maps no dice (`weapon.damage` unsupported).
- **Secondary actions aren't mapped (own entry).** Only the first action (or `Bash`) becomes the weapon detail: the underwater crossbow's 20 ft. range, the Dagger Pistol's dagger, the Axe Musket's axe and every double weapon's other end are lost; the scorpion whip's 15 ft. reach (a melee action with a range in feet) maps as a range increment.
- **Siege engines import as personal weapons (own entry):** proficiency `siege` or `misc` and handedness `assault`, `direct` or `other`.
- **No `baseItem` (own entry; resolution is #408's, verification #584's).** Variants whose `baseTypes` name another item (Heavy Crossbow (Underwater), Dwarven Heavy Pelletbow, Thorn Bow, Weighted Spear, Dagger Pistol, Kobold Tail Attachment, Colossal Siege Tower, Assault Bridge) carry their own weapon blocks.
- **Journal-page links (#738).** Heavy Crank Crossbow and Light Crank Crossbow link the Climb rules journal page (`Compendium.pf1.pf1e-rules…JournalEntryPage.ZAwjVOTwsBpZRgw4`), which the importer strips, so the full-corpus gate reports both as "unresolved imported reference" failures, as on the base. They stay held.
- No #739 gap applies, and no linked pf-items record is missing (silk rope, the light crossbow and the repeating heavy crossbow are in the pinned packs).
