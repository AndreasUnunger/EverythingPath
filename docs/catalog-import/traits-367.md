## Notable cases

- **The trait framework.** Every trait relies on character traits and the trait bonus type, which are Advanced Player's Guide rules (the sheet contract names `trait` as the APG bonus type). Each held trait records PZO1115 as an inherited rule, anchored by PCGen's APG basic trait row (`apg_abilities.lst:18`, Deft Dodger, p.328, `TYPE=Trait`). The seven drawbacks also record Ultimate Campaign's drawback rules (PZO1125, `uca_abilitycategories.lst:9`). These are the family precedent for later trait batches: PZO1115 on every trait and PZO1125 on every drawback, as minimum notice requirements (the cross-model review recommended keeping both). They don't establish complete attribution: each trait's own book chain stays held.
- **Other inherited rules.** Touched by Divinity names a mythic path (PZO1126). The Pathfinder's Exile restates the wayfinder rules it cites to _Seekers of Secrets_ (PZO9211); its linked Wayfinder record cites the _Adventurer's Guide_ (PZO1138). Thrune Loyal Agent grants fast healing (Bestiary, PZO1112, from PCGen's Bestiary rows) and Blood of Dragons low-light vision (PZO1112, as the notice guidance sets for monster abilities). Sharkchild's bestial form and Nanite Revival's nanite surge come from the skinwalker and android race records (Bestiary 5, PZO1133). Rivethun Adherent names insanity (GameMastery Guide, PZO1114). Fortified restates the fortification armor ability (CRB `cr_equipmods.lst:330`) and links the Ultimate Equipment Fortification (light) record (PZO1123). Loyalty across Lifetimes and Hex-Proof depend on the summoner's eidolon and witch hexes (PZO1115); Naive's dirty trick is APG too. 19 traits require PZO1131 because Unchained action-cost metadata (`activation.unchained`) occurs in their bound closure (see [Notice guidance](curation.md#notice-guidance), Unchained action costs, which this batch extended to a record's own actions and non-spell bodies). 18 keep it on their own action: Touched by Divinity, Questing Hellknight, The Rider, Disgraced Noble, Tireless Logic, Instant Friendship, Demon Smiter, Thrune Loyal Agent, Pitaxian Improvisation, Thrill-Seeker, Nanite Revival, Screaming Leap, Strength Foretold, Fortified, Besmara's Strength, Star Struck, Stabilizing Touch and Wendifa Apprentice; each of the 15 that bind no such linked body records the pinned line. No other entry or closure body of the batch carries that metadata. A creature, deity, region or faction a body only names as its trigger or requirement isn't treated as an inherited rule.
- **Linked bodies.** Seven bodies link eight records (Wayfinder, Light, Arcane Mark, Android, Eschew Materials, Diehard, Fortification (light), Stabilize). Each one's pinned books are required, and the assessments bind their full-corpus content closures (16 linked entries).
- **Printings.** A Shining Beacon, Touched by Divinity and Fatal Trapper cite two books, and both printings are compared. Betrayed and Missing Child cite _Curse of the Crimson Throne_ (PZO1021), but PCGen has only the Player's Guide printing (PZO9000-2S), which is kept as a lead whose book is not required. Earlier or later printings in other books (People of the River, People of the Sands, Legacy of Fire's Player's Guide) are leads too. Same-name traits with other rules are named, not required: Alchemical Prodigy, Stubborn (the ARG race-builder trait), Betrayed (a _Spymaster's Handbook_ drawback), Artifact Hunter, Wary, Bastard, Helpful and Secret-Keeper.
- **Rowless roots.** 19 traits have no PCGen row at the pin, including under alternate spellings. Each records its held root origin. Artifact Hunter and Ghoulish Affinity cite no source; their PCGen rows (Inner Sea Primer p.14, Undead Slayer's Handbook p.11) supply the only publication identity.
- **Corrected targets.** Foundry files Highlander's hilly-areas bonus under Spellcraft; the reviewed Modifier is on Stealth. ## Records and Modifiers

All 86 required note records are checked. 83 give structured Modifiers or readable Notes, and 3 are explicit covered/no-output records whose rule another record of the entry carries (Back For More's melee and ranged notes, Instant Friendship's Diplomacy copy). The separate description pass checks all 99 description records: each carries the body as a readable Note, which Arcane Malignancies splits into three parts under the sheet's 4,096-character limit. Link markup reads as the link's label.

The reviewed records add 110 Modifiers on 59 traits; with the imported changes the traits carry 138. Every typed bonus keeps its stacking type, so two traits' +1 trait bonuses on one skill don't stack, and a bonus that rises in a Situation is its new total (Highlander's +2 Stealth, Goblin Watcher's +5 Appraise).
