## Notable cases

- **The trait framework.** Every trait records PZO1115 (the APG trait framework and trait bonus type, anchored by PCGen's `apg_abilities.lst:18`), and the three drawbacks (Fey-Taken, Empty Mask, Superstitious (QC)) also PZO1125 (`uca_abilitycategories.lst:9`), as the #367 cross-model review settled. Each trait's own book chain stays held.
- **Unchained action costs (PZO1131).** The batch walks each trait's full-corpus content closure and reads every body's pinned record for `activation.unchained` (owner ruling, 2026-10-17). **10 traits** require PZO1131: 7 for their own action (Rousing Courage, Pragmatic Polytheist, Scion of Legend, Shadowsight, Corpse Dodger, Brave Words, Wardbreaker) and 5 through a linked body: Pragmatic Polytheist (Resistance) and Corpse Dodger (Hide from Undead), which also have their own, Secret Scrolls (Read Magic), Chosen of Iomedae (Light) and Wild Ferocity (the Orc Ferocity racial trait's `nonaction`). Rousing Courage's own `nonaction` cost counts too. The batch test pins the 10 keys.
- **Other inherited rules.** Cruel Child boosts the changeling's claws (PZO9280, the pinned Changeling race record). Focused Burn adds damage to the alchemist's bombs (PZO1115, the pinned Alchemist class record's bomb line). Shadowsight grants low-light vision (PZO1112, as the notice guidance and #367's Blood of Dragons set). Shadow Stalker links the fetchling's Shadow Blending (PZO9280, PZO1116, PZO1121) and Wild Ferocity links Orc Ferocity (PZO9280, PZO1110, PZO1121), so those books are required. A creature, deity, region, faction or weapon a body only names as its trigger or requirement isn't treated as an inherited rule.
- **Unresolved origins.** Curse in the Blood names the curse descriptor, and Iron Liver names drugs and alcohol. No pinned record or PCGen row in this review identifies the book that defines either, so each is a named unresolved origin.
- **Linked bodies.** Ten bodies link 15 records: Noble Born - Surtova links the other five Noble Born versions (PZO9000-6E; Orlovsky is a key of this batch, Garress a #369 key); Easy Way or the Hard Way links Favored Enemy, and Wild Ferocity and Wild Shadow link Favored Terrain (ranger, PZO1110); Wild Ferocity also links Orc Ferocity and Diehard; Pragmatic Polytheist, Secret Scrolls, Corpse Dodger and Chosen of Iomedae link the Resistance, Read Magic, Hide from Undead and Light spells (PZO1110); Shadow Stalker links Shadow Blending.
