## Rulings applied

This batch follows the family precedent of #425, #426 and #427 (Sol's reviews and fix checks) and adds no family ruling.

1. **PZO1131 on every weapon action (S-02, #736).** All 86 records with an action (every weapon, firearm and siege engine) keep `activation.unchained` on it and require the held PZO1131, so no weapon can be admitted until it clears. Three ammunition records reach it through the bodies they link: the Raining Arrow's holy water (and the bless water spell it links), the Tangleshot Arrow's tanglefoot bag (and its web, slow and black tentacles links) and the Ballista Net's net. Dueling Sword, Sword Cane Pistol and Snag Net also reach it through linked weapons. Each bound body is a PZO1131 inherited-rule entry; the full set is pinned by key in the batch test.
