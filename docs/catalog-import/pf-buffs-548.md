## Family rulings (first batch of the family)

This is the family's first batch, so each choice below is proposed as the family's precedent for review. Rulings 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 apply the #547 rulings Sol accepted for the pf1 buffs.

1. **Governing record.** A buff applies its rules "as per" the record its description links first: the pf1 Spell for a Spell Effect (also its `spellKey`), the pf1 class ability or feat for a class or combat buff, the pf1-content racial trait for a racial-trait buff. That record is the buff's rule source (`inheritedRuleSourceEvidence`), every linked record's books are required notices, and the comparison is PCGen's row of the governing record's book: spell full-text, summary and temporary-bonus rows; class-ability, feat and race-ability rows. Where the pinned PRD capture (`prd-2026-10-20`) has the class page, it is cited too (`prdComparisonEvidence`: the hunter, witch, bard and unchained rogue).
2. **A missing citation is no hold (Sol review, 2026-10-21; #241, #507).** 76 of the 97 buffs carry no `system.sources`, so their imported entries show no citation. That alone doesn't hold content that permitted evidence supports: each is assessed on its content like a cited record. 63 are confirmed (their notices or governing records may still hold them), and **Lunge** (`pf1-content/7DtG0pydiheVvIS8`) is admitted: its body, duration included, equals the admitted Core Rulebook Lunge feat and PCGen `cr_feats.lst:117`, and it needs only PZO1110. The other 13 keep independently justified holds: the five Features senses, Cover (Total) and Squeezing (large) restate rules no permitted source states (`whole-content`), as do Animal Focus (Owl) and Bestow Curse (Ability), and Weapons Against Evil's duration (minutes per caster level) contradicts both PCGen rows (rounds; Sol review of fix 1); Animal Focus (Falcon), Blessings of Luck and Resolve and Expeditious Retreat fail the gate (`broken-reference`).
3. **Spell Effects depend on their Spell.** Every linked Spell is held by its own batch, so no Spell Effect can be admitted yet; each also requires PZO1110, the Spell's books and the books of what it grants.
4. **PZO1131 through the closure.** Every Foundry spell action keeps `activation.unchained`, and so do the actions of Evil Eye, Great Cleave, the suli and race builder Elemental Assault and several racial traits: 80 of the 97 buffs reach Unchained action-cost metadata through their closure. The two Debilitating Injury buffs, whose class ability has no action, require PZO1131 for the rule itself (an unchained rogue class feature, PCGen `pu_abilities_class.lst:583`), so 82 require the held PZO1131. Both sets are pinned by key in the batch test.
5. **Monster abilities (as #541).** A buff granting a sense or a universal monster rule requires the earliest book its pinned monster-ability record or Universal Monster Rules entry lists: blindsense, blindsight, scent, low-light vision, ferocity and distraction PZO1112; see in darkness PZO1116; darkvision PZO1127.
6. **Caster level and class level.** Foundry's `@item.level` maps to nothing. A Spell Effect's reviewed Modifiers read the recorded caster level (`@casterLevel`): Divine Power, Acute Senses, Jump, Unerring Weapon, Bullet Shield, Sanctify Armor and Lay of the Land.
7. **Another creature's level is a tier (as #547).** Evil Eye depends on the witch's level, Debilitating Injury (Disoriented) on the rogue's, the four Animal Focus buffs on the hunter's (her companion's sheet can't read it). Each tier is a local Situation, the tiers are mutually exclusive, and the player selects the one that applies.
8. **Types and leaves.** Foundry's private `resist` type imports as unsupported; the reviewed Modifiers use the text's resistance bonus (Resistance, Stunning Barrier, Shield of Law, Cloak of Chaos). Armor and natural armor bonuses sit on their own leaves (#734): Sanctify Armor's enhancement on `ac.armor`, the beast talker's natural armor bonus on `ac.natural`.
9. **Shared Situations only on an exact match.** `lawfulCreatures`, `goodCreatures`, `chaoticCreatures`, `mindAffecting`, `fear`, `death` and `trip` are used where the body names that condition; everything else is local (attacks of opportunity, firearm and ranged attacks, undead, evil creatures, the one roll Guidance applies to).
10. **A buff's own activation is the toggle, not a Situation.** When the body's condition is the state the buff represents (Water Bond's "near your bonded body of water", Halo's manifested halo, a frenzy or the bestial form), the entry being on is that condition, and a Note says when to switch it on. A condition narrower than the buff's duration is a Situation: Lay of the Land's "in the affected area", Veil of Positive Energy's undead.
11. **Chosen skills and abilities stay Notes.** Inspire Competence and Bestow Insight apply to a chosen skill and Bestow Curse to a chosen ability; Spell Effect and class-feature entries record no such choice, so the bonus is a Note (an outstanding calculation). Bestow Curse is also held: its imported change fixes the -6 on Strength.
12. **Race builder links.** Elemental Assault (Cold/Electricity, Race Builder) and Frenzy (Race Builder) link ARG race builder traits that the import contract excludes, so the link is stripped and the gate fails them: held `broken-reference`.
13. **Notes.** Senses, speeds, temporary hit points, damage reduction, spell resistance, miss chances, immunities, carrying capacity, extra damage dice and play-time limits have no sheet statistic: the reviewed records state them as Notes, on the statistic they qualify where one fits. They are outstanding calculations, never holds.

## Notable cases

- **Lunge is admitted** (ruling 2): its -2 penalty to AC until your next turn equals PCGen `cr_feats.lst:117`, it needs only PZO1110, and its closure is the Lunge feat #333 admitted.
- **Inspire Heroics and Inspire Competence are dependent omissions.** Their content is confirmed against PCGen's Core Rulebook rows (`cr_abilities_class.lst:531`, `:525`) and the PRD bard page, and they need only PZO1110, but their governing class abilities (`pf1/LIFTbzT5WDcsDn0O`, `pf1/2M56kfIOws5yYtxq`) have no Attribution Assessment yet, as #547's Inspire Courage.
- **Dispel Chaos, Dispel Good, Protection From Law and Good, Shield of Law and Cloak of Chaos** apply rules their governing spells take from Dispel Evil and Protection from Evil; those Core Rulebook full-text rows (`cr_spells.lst:936`, `:1216`) are recorded as leads.
- **Veil of Positive Energy.** Its two imported +2 sacred changes (AC and saves) apply against everything; the governing Advanced Player's Guide spell and PCGen `apg_spells.lst:2107` limit both to attacks or effects created by undead. Two rows in `reviewed-change-replacements.ts` set them to 0, and the description record applies them under a local undead Situation. The batch test checks both.
