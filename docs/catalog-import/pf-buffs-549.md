## Notable cases

- **Combat Expertise is admitted.** Its body, duration ("until your next turn") included, equals the admitted Core Rulebook feat and PCGen `cr_feats.lst:33`; it needs only PZO1110 and its closure is the admitted feat. The imported changes carry the melee attack penalty and the dodge bonus (`1 + floor(@bab / 4)`); the body also puts the penalty on combat maneuver checks, which no imported change covers, so the description record adds the same untyped penalty on CMB.
- **Magic Fang** grants its +1 to one natural weapon or unarmed strike; Foundry's changes put it on every melee attack and all weapon damage. Two rows in `reviewed-change-replacements.ts` set them to 0, and the description record applies the +1 on melee attack and damage rolls under a local Situation for the affected weapon (as #548 did for Veil of Positive Energy). The batch test checks both.
- **Caster-level Modifiers** (`@casterLevel`): Anticipate Peril (`min(5, CL)` insight on initiative), Wrathful Mantle (`min(5, floor(CL / 4))` resistance), Magic Fang, Greater and Magic Weapon, Greater (`min(5, floor(CL / 4))` enhancement under the affected weapon's Situation; Foundry's `max(1, …)` has no cap), Magic Vestment (on the armor leaf or the shield leaf under mutually exclusive local Situations, #734). Greater magic fang's alternative +1 on all natural weapons is its own exclusive Situation.
- **Foundry's private types.** Holy Aura and Unholy Aura (`resist`) get a reviewed +4 resistance bonus on saves; Blessing of Fervor (Atk, AC, Ref) and Channel Vigor (Limbs) (`haste`) get the text's untyped attack bonus and dodge bonuses to AC and Reflex, haste's +1s coming from the Core Rulebook haste row (`cr_spells.lst:1045`).
- **Notes become Modifiers where the sheet has the statistic:** Channel Vigor (Torso) on concentration, Death Knell's +1 on caster level, Jitterbugs' -4 on the seven Dexterity-based skills, Threefold Aspect (Elderly)'s -2 Strength and Dexterity (`strPen`, `dexPen`).
- **Righteous Might**: Foundry's DR formula gives 15 from caster level 15; the body and PCGen `cr_spells.lst:759` give 10, which the Note follows. Its -2 Dexterity is typed size by Foundry and untyped in the text; recorded as a discrepancy (a size change's penalty doesn't stack with another size change).
- **Angelic Aspect (Lesser)** links the Spell "Angelic Aspect, Lesser"; the name match fails, so the gate fails it (`broken-reference`, as #548's Blessings of Luck and Resolve). **Animal Focus (Frog)** is typed `subType: spell` with no Spell (as #548's Falcon). **Elemental Assault (Acid|Fire, Race Builder)** and **Stench Aura** link ARG race builder traits the import excludes. These five are the batch's gate failures.
- **Skinwalker buffs.** The three beast talker buffs (CON, STR, Darkvision) are confirmed against PCGen's Inner Sea Races row (`isr_abilities_race.lst:1217`). The nine other Change Shape buffs restate the base change shape or a heritage, which PCGen files only in its Core Essentials compilation: held `whole-content`, as #548.
- **Racial traits PCGen doesn't encode** (Inspiration of Bestiary 6; Iron Within (Speed), Small Quarter Ally and Stoic Defender of Heroes from the Fringe) are held `whole-content`. Elemental Assault (Cold, Suli) and Joyous Companion are confirmed (Qadira and Legacy of Dragons rows) and held on notices.
- **Combat states and senses** (Charging, Climbing, Cover (Soft), Flanking, both Squeezing records, Low-Light Vision, Tremorsense 30', Truesight 120') restate core rules or monster abilities no permitted pinned source states: held `whole-content`, as #548's Cover (Total) and senses. Charging starts with a Paizo FAQ entry, which isn't a permitted source.
