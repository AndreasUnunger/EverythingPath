## Importer and data gaps

Reported, not fixed (owner amendment 2: no importer code changes). None of them is an attribution hold.

1. **Material-only armor blocks import as armor (own entry; 13 records).** `mapArmor` (`scripts/catalog/details.ts:416`) maps any `system.armor` object, and the clothing and wondrous item records carry one holding only a material (`{material: {base: {value: leather}}}`). So the ring of protection, cloak, headband, the two belts, the three amulets of natural armor and the four outfits import an armor block `{slot: armor, category: "", bonus: 0}`: on a sheet they add a +0 armor-type row on `ac.armor`, count as worn armor (a synthesist's fused form drops them, with their Modifiers: `src/lib/character-sheet-combined-form.ts:91`), and accept armor Item Abilities. The bracers of armor's +4 (from `system.armor.value`) is the intended bonus.

   **Fixed by #762 (2026-10-21).** `mapArmor` now maps only real armor: a material-only block imports nothing, and a non-armor item's `armor.value` (the bracers' +4) imports as an armor-bonus Modifier on `ac.armor`, not worn armor.
