# Core Rulebook condition curation

Ticket #311 supplies the complete finite inventory of 34 locally authored CRB conditions. Foundry has no conditions pack. The definitions live in [`reviewed-conditions.json`](../../src/lib/catalog/data/reviewed-conditions.json), under the application catalog data, and cite the agreed [conditions corpus](../ai/pf1-core-rules/pf1-crb-conditions.md). That corpus is preserved byte for byte, including its retrieval date, source correction and ambiguity list. This curation used the agreed offline corpus; it does not claim another web retrieval or printed-page verification.

## Inventory and supported behavior

Every definition has a stable key and per-condition rule identity, numeric Modifiers, readable Situational Notes, source sections/citations and an explicit list of quantities that are not calculated. Conditions are Temporary Effects. An inactive condition contributes nothing; permanent projections exclude its numbers, structural effects and Notes, and while-active dependencies on it stay conditional.

The numbers below are leaf contributions, before ordinary ability/BAB/equipment composition. All penalties are untyped. Invisible and Prone's unnamed bonuses are untyped; Grappled's specific CMD bonus is circumstance.

The exact readable rules and omitted numeric quantities are in each definition and in [`conditions-admission.json`](conditions-admission.json). Unsupported does not mean zero: no negative-level count or score override is guessed. In particular, Helpless/Paralyzed and inherited helpless states leave ordinary score totals intact while explaining that effective zero scores and their consequences are not calculated. There is no HP pool, round counter, duration management or combat-state control.

This bounded reviewed local resource declares its shared, flat Situation vocabulary (separate from the imported-note overlay vocabulary): `opposed-perception`, `sight-based`, `reaction-check`, `sighted-opponent`, `grapple-or-escape`, `grapple-while-invisible`, `ranged-attack` and `melee-attack`. A requested Situation recomputes stacking through the same public resolver. None implies another. Entry-level prose uses local Situations named after its condition. Grappled's CMD circumstance uses the requested Situation that explicitly includes invisibility. Its ordinary CMB total includes the −2 penalty; the grapple-or-escape Situation adds +2 to cancel that condition's penalty for those checks.

## Combinations and open rules questions

The [CRB Conditions introduction](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/glossary.html#conditions) applies all conditions and selects the most severe when effects cannot combine. The following readings are explicit. Items 1, 6, 7 and 8 record the owner's 2026-10-13 rulings (#725); the others do not resolve the source's ambiguity beyond the agreed literal implementation. All twelve questions in the [corpus ambiguity list](../ai/pf1-core-rules/pf1-crb-conditions.md#7-ambiguities-and-gaps) remain recorded.

1. **Panicked versus Fear.** The [Conditions: Panicked entry](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/glossary.html#panicked) lists −2 saves/skills/ability checks without attacks. The same [glossary's Fear section](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/glossary.html) says panicked creatures are also shaken, whose entry includes attacks. The owner ruled on 2026-10-13 (#725) that Panicked applies all of Shaken's modifiers, so the resolver gives Panicked −2 on attack rolls, saves and skills; ability checks stay unmodeled, as for Shaken. This applies to a recorded Panicked and to Panicked reached by fear escalation; the escalated rows are replaced, so the −2 never repeats.
2. **Apply all versus severity.** The Conditions introduction and [Fear section](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/glossary.html) describe different combination mechanisms. Separate untyped penalties stack under the established [documented stacking contract](../pf-character-sheet-data-model.md#stacking), resolved in [#209](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/209), so Shaken + Sickened gives −4 and Entangled + Grappled gives −8 Dex before the floor. Fear entries instead escalate: two Shaken become Frightened; Shaken + Frightened or two Frightened become Panicked. Repeated Fatigued becomes Exhausted; Exhausted supersedes Fatigued. Pinned supersedes all Grappled numbers/conditionals under the two appendix entries' nonstacking text. The open question is how broadly the general severity sentence should constrain otherwise stackable penalties. Apart from the invisible CMD Situation in item 6, no inherited Grappled numbers are invented for Pinned.
3. **Effective zero scores.** Conditions: Helpless and Paralyzed state effective Dex 0 (and Str 0 for Paralyzed); the [Ability Score Damage, Penalty, and Drain section](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/glossary.html) describes ordinary penalties with a minimum of 1. An effective-zero override is a distinct operation the prepared resolver does not implement. Its numbers are honestly unmodeled, including the inherited helpless behavior of Dying, Stable, Unconscious and Petrified. Ordinary condition score penalties are capped after other score contributions; they never lift an already sub-1 drain/manual score or clamp a permanent score.
4. **Dex, dodge and CMD.** The condition appendix mentions Dex loss to AC, while [CRB combat Armor Class and Combat Maneuver Defense](https://legacy.aonprd.com/coreRulebook/combat.html) provide the established defense composition. Positive Dex and positive dodge bonuses are removed from AC/touch AC once, with attribution to a condition. Negative Dex remains. Flat-Footed also loses positive Dex to CMD and retains dodge there. Other condition Dex denials do not automatically deny CMD Dex. The appendix's silence on these other CMD implications remains open.
5. **Unspecified checks and types.** Blinded's “most” Str/Dex skills gives no exact exception list, so that −4 is prose rather than a universal physical-skill penalty. Opposed Perception, reaction checks, sighted opponents and grapple/escape distinctions remain explicit Situations. Bonus typing is absent except Grappled's circumstance bonus. The unresolved Broken armor/enhancement interpretation, nonlethal thresholds, fatigue triggers, adaptation and other item/creature/event quantities are preserved in the corpus and definition Notes rather than guessed.
6. **Pinned and invisibility.** Pinned replaces Grappled's effects under the corpus's literal nonstacking reading. The owner ruled on 2026-10-13 (#725) that an invisible pinned creature is limited like a grappled one: invisibility gives only the +2 circumstance bonus to CMD to avoid being grappled. While Pinned, an Invisible row's attack bonus against sighted opponents is suppressed with the Pinned row as its explanation, and Pinned's own definition offers the +2 circumstance CMD as the same selectable `grapple-while-invisible` Situation Grappled offers. Pinned inherits no other Grappled number.
7. **Grappled's invisible CMD Situation.** The corpus gives the grappled creature +2 circumstance CMD against being grappled if it becomes invisible. The implementation exposes this as the selectable `grapple-while-invisible` Situation; it does not apply the bonus automatically when an Invisible row is active. The owner confirmed this on 2026-10-13 (#725), and Pinned offers the same Situation. This concerns the grappled creature's invisibility, not the grappler's.
8. **Non-escalating Shaken sources.** Two Shaken rows become Frightened under the agreed corpus's literal fear chain. The resolver has no source-specific non-escalation distinction. The owner confirmed this on 2026-10-13 (#725). No exception is inferred from a condition's source.

Replacement is calculation-only: it does not mutate the recorded rows or their active flags. Effects report `replacedBy` as the surviving sheet-row ID. Original Catalog Entry IDs remain the identity for while-active dependencies, including recorded rows whose numerical contribution is replaced. Ordinary stacking still uses per-condition Sources; the entire CRB is not one stacking Source. Invisible's attack contribution is explicitly suppressed while Grappled or Pinned, with the surviving Grappled or Pinned row as its explanation (#725).

## Sheet and UI interface

The existing `createSheetEntry`/`editSheetEntry`/`removeSheetEntry` interface is reused. The condition detail accepts an optional bounded `conditionKey`; a selected reviewed key supplies canonical name, Modifiers, per-condition rule identity/Source and CRB sources on the server. Conflicting authored mechanics are refused. Editing an existing keyed condition retains that key; explicitly supplying `{ kind: 'condition' }` detaches it to a custom definition and an independent Source. Existing custom conditions continue to work.

`calculateCharacterSheet` and both projections include `conditionEffects`: sheet-row ID, effective condition key/name, optional replacement row, readable Notes and unmodeled quantities. Permanent effects are empty. Numeric breakdowns explain applied/conditional/suppressed contributions; limited ability penalties have an explicit minimum-score correction so their totals remain the sum of their applied contributions.

The existing form controller exposes the 34 options, selection and explicit customization while preserving keyed edits. The presentation handoff is `/tmp/ep-orch/briefs/311/ui-contract.md`: the picker and Notes/unmodeled display still belong to the separate UI agent. No presentation components were authored in this ticket.

## Admission, legal notices and release resources

The public operator adapter is [`scripts/catalog/conditions.ts`](../../scripts/catalog/conditions.ts). `buildConditionCatalogArtifact()` produces actual candidates, resource hashes and local inventory rows, without fabricating upstream extraction fields; `appendConditionResources(artifact)` combines them with an upstream extraction. `assessCatalogAdmission` handles the combined candidates with the ordinary accepted content/mapping/evidence/notice bindings. The 34 Attribution Assessments are committed static reviews, not rebuilt automatically when content changes. Modified Modifiers, structural flags, Notes, unmodeled quantities, evidence or notices reopen review. Missing local inventory rows fail admission.

The offline conditions command reads the complete assessment, evidence, registry and legal-resource JSON files and validates them with the same schemas as the application's reviewed data. It reads JSON directly so the TypeScript loader does not compile the full evidence corpus into JavaScript. The full-data command test compares the entire output with the committed report; the separate resolver-edit fixture keeps only the Conditions' evidence.

`CRB` (alias `PZO1110`) uses the exact `Pathfinder RPG Core Rulebook` Section 15 line already preserved in the pinned pf1-content legal compilation. Its provenance states the offline comparison and does not claim printed-book verification. OGL and SRD lines remain in the already shipped legal resources, not duplicated in the registry. Both inherited upstream notices, the project notice, Section 8 and the Community Use notice remain present. The legal route includes CRB among required notices. This is notice accounting under the project's existing distribution policy, not a new legal-sufficiency conclusion.

The adapter reports all four current byte SHA-256 hashes in `catalog.localResources`, so any resource edit changes the release extraction fingerprint. Unrelated shared-calculator edits leave accepted condition content stable; edits to the conditions resolver, definitions, their calculated Modifiers, structural flags, Notes, unmodeled quantities, corpus, evidence or notices still reopen attribution review. The accepted trade-off is that condition behavior in `character-sheet.ts`, including `conditionApplies` and suppression, is not review-bound. The upstream-only extraction interface stays unchanged. Full-builder preview must opt in with `--conditions`; this flag does not bypass the upstream curation or notice gates.
