## Family rulings (first batch of the family; for review)

This is the family's first batch, so these choices are proposed as the family's precedent:

1. **Admission bar.** An item is admitted when a PCGen Core Rulebook row (`cr_equip_arms_armor.lst`) is a complete direct comparison: its literal `DESC` is the pinned body (differences only in hyphenation and apostrophes) and every statistic (armor or shield bonus, maximum Dexterity bonus, armor check penalty, arcane spell failure, cost, weight) matches both the pinned record and the mapped detail, and the record cites only PZO1110, has no action and links nothing. Chain Shirt (`:40`) and Buckler (`:109`) meet it; their assessments are `confirmed` with PZO1110 (the reviewed CRB Registry identity) and no hold.
2. **Core Rulebook items with another wording stay held.** Fifteen records cite only PZO1110 and match their CRB row's statistics, but their bodies are not the CRB wording PCGen records: Banded Mail, Breastplate, Chainmail, Full Plate, Half-Plate, Hide Armor, Leather Armor, Padded Armor, Scale Mail, Splint Mail, Studded Leather Armor and the four light and heavy wooden and steel shields. No permitted source at the pins carries that wording (PCGen's Ultimate Equipment rows have no `DESC`; the PRD capture has no equipment pages), so its origin is untraced and the whole content is held. This is an attribution hold on an untraced body, not the "partial PCGen comparison" the 2026-10-13 policy (#735) says is not by itself a hold reason: the mechanics match, the text's book is unknown. PCGen's Ultimate Equipment row for each one is recorded as a lead, and PZO1123 is required and held. Tower Shield likewise (its body adds "(see cover, Core Rulebook 195)" to the CRB text, and it cites Ultimate Equipment p.9).
3. **Books.** Every record requires its cited books, the books of the PCGen rows it follows (comparison and leads) and its linked bodies' books; rows whose statistics the record does not follow are rejected and their books not required (Hide Shirt's Curse of the Crimson Throne row; Chain Shirt's Ultimate Equipment reprint, since its body is the CRB wording). Codes without a reviewed Registry identity are held as `armors-and-shields-424-notice-<code>-complete-chain`, 15 holds registered in `held-notices.json`.
4. **PZO1131** for Unchained action-cost metadata anywhere in the bound closure: 14 records, all on their own actions (the 12 shields' Bash actions, Tower Shield's total cover and Armor of Insults' daily use), Armor of Insults also through the bound confusion spell. The admitted Chain Shirt and Buckler have no action.
5. **Records with no citation** (37) keep a whole-content hold: PCGen rows identify the printing by statistics only, and their books are required. Armored Kilt and Ice Coat (uncited) and Poisoner's Buckler (PZO9491) have no PCGen data row at all; their root origin is unresolved.
6. **Description pass.** Every in-scope record has exactly one checked description record (66), as the batch-ownership sweep requires. The 30 bodies that state a rule (all 12 prose-pass candidates among them) get reviewed outputs: their Notes are the body's own sentences, on the statistic they qualify where one fits (AC, attack, Swim), and five Situation-gated Modifiers (Buckler's −1 attack while its arm wields a weapon; +2 circumstance on Bluff to feint (Dancing Scarves), Stealth in snowy environments (Ice Coat), Diplomacy and Intimidate to influence the armor's country (Parade Armor), one Situation for both). They add no Note or Modifier, so no binding changed. No Situation is shared (none matches exactly).
7. **Gear review.** Each record lists its statistics against PCGen and the mapped detail, its shield bash, Base Item, defaults and end state (`mechanicsReview.gearReview`). Speed is not on the sheet, so medium and heavy armor speed is an outstanding calculation, never a hold.

## Notable cases

- **Chain Shirt and Buckler (admitted).** Both are the CRB row's `DESC` word for word (Buckler differs only in "off-hand"/"offhand", "two-handed"/"twohanded" and apostrophes) with every statistic equal. Chain Shirt's description record is covered/no-output (its body is descriptive). Buckler's record gives its −1 on attack rolls as two untyped Modifiers under local Situations, one on melee attacks ("while your buckler arm wields a melee weapon (an off-hand weapon, or helping to wield a two-handed weapon)") and one on ranged attacks ("while your buckler arm wields a ranged weapon other than a bow or crossbow"), so the body's bow and crossbow exemption is never penalised (review fix: the first version's single Situation on all attacks covered bows too). The penalty stacks with the off-hand and two-weapon penalties, as the body says and keeps the lost-AC and no-bash sentences as Notes on AC and attack. Worn with Dexterity 18 and the Proficiencies, the pair gives +4 armor, +1 shield, AC 19, armor check penalty −3 and 25% spell failure; without the Proficiencies, −3 on attack rolls.
- **Tower Shield.** Its imported −2 untyped attack change is the body's encumbrance penalty and the CRB row's (`BONUS:COMBAT|TOHIT|-2`), so it is not curated twice (the contract's "Curation Overlay Modifier" is already supplied by the import). Total cover is a Note on AC.
- **Elven Chain** follows the CRB p.464 row word for word but cites Ultimate Equipment p.126 (PZO1123, held), links Chainmail (held) and maps as medium armor with an armor check penalty of 3: the body treats it as light armor in all ways and states −2 (the record's masterwork flag is dropped; see gaps). Listed for review; no overlay field can correct an armor block.
- **Armor of Insults** is a +1 studded leather (its `magic` default is enhancement 1), cites PZO1123 and PZO1115, links Studded Leather Armor, Craft Magic Arms and Armor (admitted, #330) and confusion (whose action keeps Unchained metadata). - **Statistics that differ from PCGen**, each recorded: Dancing Scarves (armor bonus 2 always; the AA2 row has 0 and the body grants +2 only after moving 10 feet), Gray Maiden Plate (armor check penalty 6, as full plate per its body; the AG row says 5), Leaf Armor (500 gp; the ISWG row 350 gp without the masterwork cost), Rosewood Armor (50 gp; the AA row 0), Spider-Silk Bodysuit (10% spell failure; AA2 0%), Wooden Snarlshield (50 gp and 20 lb., the steel snarlshield's; both PCGen rows give 37 gp and 15 lb.). ## Importer and data gaps

- **Fixed by #760 (landed separately):** the shield slot and the light-armor category; see Importer prerequisite.
- **Masterwork without an enhancement (own entry).** `mapItemMagic` maps `magic` only when the record has an enhancement, so `masterwork: true` alone is dropped: Elven Chain keeps an armor check penalty of 3 for the body's −2; Leaf Armor and Poisoner's Buckler lose the flag (their penalty is already 0).
- **Base Items (own entry; resolution is #408's, verification #584's).** The importer maps no `baseItem` from `baseTypes`: Armor of Insults ("Studded Leather Armor") and Elven Chain ("Chainmail") carry their own armor blocks, so their statistics are right, but no Base Item link is imported. The mundane records here have `baseTypes` naming themselves (Gray Maiden, Hellknight and Lamellar variants name another item and carry their own statistics).
- **Shield bash fallbacks (own entry).** The Bash action maps handedness `light` for every shield (no `weaponSubtype`; a heavy shield bash is one-handed) and proficiency `shield` from `subType` (PCGen types the klar bash martial and the war-shield and madu bash exotic). The weapon detail is outside this batch's admissions (both admitted items have no bash).
- **Armor block corrections (own entry).** The overlay can't replace armor block values (`reviewed-change-replacements` covers change formulas only), so Dancing Scarves' always-on +2 and Elven Chain's medium category stay as imported and are listed for review.
- **Material (own entry).** `armor.material` (for example Elven Chain's mithral, Poisoner's Buckler's) is not mapped; the specific records bake its effects into their numbers.
- No #738 or #739 gap applies, and no linked pf-items record is missing.
