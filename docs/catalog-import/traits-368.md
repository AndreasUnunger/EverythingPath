## Notable cases

- **The trait framework.** Every trait records PZO1115 (the APG trait framework and trait bonus type, anchored by PCGen's `apg_abilities.lst:18`), and the two drawbacks (Tainted Spirit, Impatient) also PZO1125 (`uca_abilitycategories.lst:9`), as the minimum the #367 cross-model review settled. They don't establish complete attribution; each trait's own book chain stays held.
- **Unchained action costs (PZO1131).** Following the owner's ruling (2026-10-17), Unchained action-cost metadata (`activation.unchained`) anywhere in a trait's bound content closure requires the held PZO1131. The batch finds it systematically: it walks each trait's full-corpus content closure and reads every body's pinned record. **24 traits** require it: 21 for their own action (Amiable Blunder, Denial of Fate, Numerian Fluid Indulger, Venom Resistance, The Snoop, Living Bulwark, Empyreal Focus, Curator of Mystic Secrets, Transmuter of Korada, Jadwiga Medicine, Barroom Talespinner, Restless Hunger, Lessons of Chaldira, Varki Landspeaker, Burst of Speed, Irritable Diplomat, Linked Surge, Omen, Spirit Touched, Planar Escapee, Numerologist), and 3 only through a linked body: Radiant Healer (Light), Whip Specialist (Whip) and Beastkin (Speak with Animals). - **Other inherited rules.** Stolen Fury names a mythic path (PZO1126). Numerian Fluid Indulger uses the Numerian fluid table it cites to _Numeria, Land of Fallen Stars_ (PZO9270). Monster Stalker grants a limited scent (PZO1112, from the pinned Scent monster ability, as the notice guidance sets). Augmented Spell Poisons and Born in the Light extend the vishkanya toxic and dhampir light sensitivity racial traits (PZO9280, the pinned Inner Sea Races race records). Whip Specialist's linked Whip cites Ultimate Equipment (PZO1123). A creature, deity, region, faction or weapon a body only names as its trigger or requirement isn't treated as an inherited rule; Dungeon Dweller's suggestion to play an Advanced Race Guide race isn't a rule, so PZO1121 isn't required.
- **Unresolved origins.** Acclimatize Alignment's planar alignment penalties, Brigh's Insight's madness effects and Alchemical Breath's alchemical coal are only named; no pinned record or PCGen row in this review identifies their books, so each is a named unresolved origin.

- **Corrected targets and types.** Whip Specialist, Scion of Goblinblood and Clockwork Engineer's bonuses are on damage, not attack. Flotsam's +2 is untyped and on Reflex only.
