## Racial traits

Decided by [Decide how racial traits live on a Character Sheet](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/232).

- **Rules sources.** The _Advanced Player's Guide_ and _Advanced Race Guide_ rules for alternate racial traits and subraces are admitted. An alternate racial trait is exchanged for one or more standard racial traits, and "you cannot exchange the same racial trait more than once" (APG). Neither book defines "alters". The _Advanced Race Guide_ race builder (race points) is out of scope. A custom race is a campaign or character Catalog Copy of a race.
- **Sheet entries.** Racial Traits are `racialTrait` sheet entries (see "Grants and dormant entries").
  - The race's `racialTraits` lists its standard traits, which are Grants of the race, like class features. Changing the race makes the old race's Grants and its alternates dormant, and the new race's traits appear.
  - An alternate is a Selection. The standard traits its `replaces` names go dormant with their state, and come back when it is removed or turned off.
  - Racial Traits are permanent entries, so the ability score trait counts for Militia Character Facts as the race's adjustments did.
- **Modifiers sit on the traits.** A Modifier lives on exactly one entry, so nothing counts twice. A race entry keeps its racial Hit Dice, progression, size and creature type; its Modifiers come from its Racial Traits. The importer ([#719](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/719); see [the catalog import](../catalog-import/README.md#racial-trait-grants)):
  - drops race changes that a standard trait also carries, keeping the trait's version when they differ. When the trait's version does not map to a Modifier and the race's does, the race's version moves onto the trait instead;
  - applies a reviewed race note to the standard trait whose note covers the same statistic, and moves the reviewed race description outputs listed in `reviewedDescriptionRehomes` onto their traits;
  - leaves anything else on the race and lists it in the import report until the Curation Overlay moves it.
  - Since [#720](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/720) it also maps the race's size and imports natural armor as a natural armor bonus, except Granite Skin (Oread)'s racial bonus to natural armor, which stays racial. It reports speeds and spell resistance, which the sheet has no statistic for (see [Race facts](../catalog-import/README.md#race-facts)).
  - Most races have no ability score trait record: of the Core Rulebook races only the human, half-elf and half-orc do. Since [#721](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/721), each of the 72 races whose fixed ability changes no pinned trait owns (the elf, dwarf, gnome and halfling, the orc and kobold, and most others) has a reviewed ability score trait, `Ability Score Modifiers (<Race>)`. Its Modifiers are exactly the race record's own ability changes, with the same values, targets and racial type, and it is one of the race's standard traits, like a pinned one. It takes the race record's citation, notice holds and Attribution Assessment, and its description is the race description's own ability line. The races with no trait folder (gnoll, lizardfolk, ogre and both lashunta records) get it as their only Racial Trait; their other Modifiers stay on the race. See [Reviewed ability score traits](../catalog-import/README.md#racial-trait-grants).

  So an alternate that replaces the ability modifiers makes that trait dormant, with the adjustments. Orc Atavism, Kindred-Raised and Witchborn replace "Ability Score Modifiers", and Tree-Born the gathlain's "Constitution Penalty"; a reviewed alias of the pinned trait resolves each name. Their own adjustments take over, so the totals are the text's: Witchborn's map as they are, and reviewed rules map Orc Atavism's `base` +2 Strength and Tree-Born's `base` +2 Dexterity as racial, Keen Kitsune's `untypedPerm` changes as racial, and Orc Atavism's −2 and Kindred-Raised's +2 as ability choices. An ability score trait is replaced only by an alternate whose own ability changes map to Modifiers (raw, by a reviewed rule, or curated), so no base adjustment goes dormant without its replacement. Dual Talent maps two different +2 choices and replaces the ordinary Human ability trait, Bonus Feat and Skilled. Its first choice retains `choice`; its optional `secondChoice` is saved independently. Duplicates warn and contribute once. An alternate that names a trait with no record, such as Arctic Elf's "Base Statistics", stays unresolved: it removes no race Modifier, and the player chooses what it replaces.

- **Ability of choice.** A "+2 to one ability score of your choice" trait (human, half-elf, half-orc and others; Orc Atavism's −2 to one mental score) has an `ability.$choice` Modifier. The sheet entry's `choice` names the ability. Until it is chosen, the Modifier contributes nothing and the field shows a blue outline. Restrictions such as "a mental ability score" stay prose. The importer sets it for the human, half-elf and half-orc traits, from their untargeted `base` change mapped as `racial`, and since #721 for Orc Atavism (−2) and Kindred-Raised (+2), from their untargeted `untyped` change; Dual Talent uses the same Modifier with `abilityChoiceCount: 2`, applying it to each distinct saved choice.
- **Facts the traits carry.** Each fact sits on the trait, so an alternate that replaces the trait makes it dormant, and the fact with it. A reviewed rule keyed by the pinned trait identity sets the Bonus Feat, Adaptability, Multitalented and ability-choice facts (`scripts/catalog/racial-trait-rules.ts`).
  - Bonus Feat (human): `grantsSlots`, from Foundry's `bonusFeats` change.
  - Adaptability (half-elf): a slot whose `feats` is Skill Focus. The Skill Focus link is on the Half-Elf race record's `links.supplements`, not on the trait.
  - Skilled (human): `bonusSkillRanksPerLevel`, from Foundry's `bonusSkillRanks` change.
  - Multitalented (half-elf): `favoredClassCount: 2`. A Character's favored class count is 1 unless an active Racial Trait sets 2. So Arcane Training, which replaces Multitalented, brings it back to 1. Its arcane-only restriction stays prose.
  - Weapon familiarity (dwarf, elf and others): `proficiencies`, from the names in the trait's own `weaponProf` that match a pinned weapon base type (see "Proficiencies"). The trait's list is the one kept: it is fuller than the elf race record's and spelled differently from the dwarf's. Gnome weapon familiarity names no weapon, and every "treat X weapons as martial" clause stays prose.
- **Standard and alternate.** The pf1-content racial traits pack marks these only by folder.
  - Standard-folder records are standard.
  - For the 22 races with a flat folder, a record is standard unless it has a "Replaced Trait" header.
  - Every record with a header is an alternate.
- **Replacement links.** The importer builds `replaces` from the "Replaced Trait(s)" header. It matches names and `@UUID` links against the same race's standard traits, ignoring a "(Race)" suffix. In the pinned import, 745 of 861 alternates match fully after #740.
  - Unmatched names go to the import report: subrace "Base Statistics", category words such as "Speed", and typos. A one-off Curation Overlay pass resolves them before launch, and no gate fails the import. A reviewed reason goes with some of them (see "Subraces").
  - An unresolved alternate imports with an empty `replaces` and lists every name its header gives in `unresolvedReplacements`, so adding it replaces nothing and the player turns the replaced trait off by hand.
  - "Alters X" reads like an Archetype's "alters": it replaces X, and the alternate's own text takes over.
- **Subraces.** A subrace is an alternate Racial Trait, not a race of its own.
  - A Subraces-folder record replaces "Base Statistics", pf1-content's own label; no published "replaces" sentence stands behind it. What it means depends on the record (#721):
    - The 37 heritage subraces (the aasimar, tiefling, dhampir and skinwalker heritages, and the elemental subraces of the ifrit, oread, sylph and undine) state their own ability line, such as Angel-Blooded's "+2 Str, +2 Cha" (_Blood of Angels_ p. 21). For these, "Base Statistics" is the parent's ability score trait. Checked curation maps 28 lines; #740 maps seven remaining exact native lines under description-hash guards. Ragebred and Scaleheart remain explicitly unresolved source conflicts with manual fallback, pending publisher-confirmed ability values and citations; their parent ability trait stays active. Skinwalker shapechanged adjustments use local Situations. Native/comparison disagreements and source/notice holds remain explicit in the drafted #740 source proposals. A changed or unstructured ability line keeps the parent trait active.
    - Oversized Goblins consumes its raw deltas and replaces the complete Goblin ability trait with +2 Strength, +2 Dexterity and −2 Charisma, and Medium size (_Monster Codex_ p. 104). Removing it restores the original adjustments and size.
    - The racial subtypes are covered below.
  - Each Subrace Standard record, such as `Skilled (Tiefling - Beastbrood)`, is an alternate replacing the parent's trait of the same name.
  - Nothing bundles a subrace with its overrides, and no check matches them: the player adds the ones they want.
  - The _Advanced Race Guide_'s racial subtypes are named combinations of alternates (Cosmopolitan is Heart of the Streets plus Focused Study), so they need nothing. They keep the base race's ability scores: Arctic Elf's "Base Statistics" (ARG p. 23) leaves the elf's ability score trait active. The _Heroes from the Fringe_ subtypes are the same.
- **Counting as another race.** `countsAsRaces` holds race rule identities and can be set on any Catalog Entry, because only prose says it.
  - Elf Blood counts as elf and human, and Orc Blood as human and orc. The importer sets these two from its reviewed rules; the Curation Overlay sets the others. Replacing them changes the counting automatically.
  - Orc Atavism counts as orc only ("not also humans").
  - Dragon Soul's "either elves or humans" is `oneOf`, and the sheet entry's `choice` picks the race.
  - The feat Half-Drow Paragon counts as drow.
  - It feeds `race` prerequisite clauses and the check for a race trait of another race (see "Rules checks"). Favored class options stay unchecked free text.
