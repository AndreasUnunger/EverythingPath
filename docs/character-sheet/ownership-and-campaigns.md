## Ownership and campaigns

Decided in [Decide how Characters exist outside a campaign, and the app's home](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/212), amended by [Decide Character ownership when campaign access changes](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/245); see [ADR 0002](../adr/0002-characters-owned-by-users-move-between-campaigns.md).

Prepared in #260: owner-private and shared campaign Full Character creation, private access/deletion, campaign archive commands and independent `/characters/<id>` routes. Campaign and owner fields are optional in storage; ordinary private creation always records the authenticated owner. Creation and sheet writes remain limited to isolated fixtures (`campaign.e2eFixture`, or server-seeded `user.characterSheetDemo` / `character.sheetDemo` for private demos), with the migration Write Gate still enforced. Characters navigation is implemented in #297: every sheet uses its independent URL, owned Characters are grouped across accessible organizations, and campaign Characters and the militia ledger have distinct pages. #300 adds campaign ownership reassignment, protected owner/member display projections and the authoritative current-owner departure check. Legacy ownership-only edits preserve flat militia authority; prepared-sheet ownership writes retain fixture restrictions and warning reconciliation. Prepared movement in #316 rechecks departure authority at publication, preserves scoped homebrew and reconciles militia assignments and Companion Relationships atomically. #703 fences a prepared move when ownership is reassigned during preparation, including A to B and back to A: ownership changed, so the prepared request is stale, and stale jobs cannot undo reassignment (PRD 14). The requester starts a new move. #706 handles organization membership loss through the same move machinery; see "Losing organization access" below. #708 handles account deletion; see "Deleting an account" below. #710 handles campaign and organization deletion; see "Deleting a campaign or organization through the app" and "An organization deleted externally" below. #711 extends prepared movement and trusted returns to recursively active same-owner Companion groups, publishing every member together. Any member’s changed ownership, campaign or eligible group fences the entire pending move. Production activation remains a follow-up; see the implementation inventory for [Characters navigation](../character-seam-inventory.md#characters-navigation-297) and [campaign ownership reassignment](../character-seam-inventory.md#campaign-ownership-reassignment-300).

- **Character Owner.** The current owning user, initially the creator. Outside a campaign, only the owner can see and edit the Character. Inside a campaign, everyone with campaign access can edit it; ownership supplies authority to leave or move, not extra editing permissions. Campaign access is organization membership, with no separate campaign membership model.
- **Reassigning ownership.** Any current campaign member can transfer ownership to any current member, including themselves, without the current owner's or recipient's approval. This deliberately permits taking ownership and then leaving with the Character. The recipient must have a surviving account and current campaign access when the transfer is applied. Outside a campaign, ownership cannot be transferred.
  - **Prepared implementation (#300).** Character list and sheet responses project `{ userId, name, isMine } | null` for the authorized caller. `isActive` remains the archive state. Optional stored `ownerLastOperationId` correlates assignment feedback and does not grant access. The indexed `organizationMembership` directory is maintained transactionally and backfilled in gated bounded batches; current `user.orgIds` remains authoritative for membership and stale/deleted candidates are rechecked before display. The current-owner departure helper is reserved for #316's final write transaction, with no public departure-check endpoint.
- **Linked Companion groups (#711).** The owner moves the requested Character and its recursively active same-owner Companions together, up to 16 Characters including the requested one. Inactive links and different owners stop traversal. A voluntary Companion move never pulls its associated Character along. Every member prepares its own complete homebrew closure; final publication selects all campaign transitions, departure corrections and relationship/fact reconciliation in one transaction. Separation preserves interrupted history, and compatible placement/support restores eligible interrupted relationships without displacing a newer active association. Preview hides inaccessible staying endpoints. A resource refusal keeps the complete group in place. Trusted departure and campaign-deletion steps choose the highest eligible associated Character first, irrespective of index order, and continue each other’s grouped return for the same owner. Account deletion still preserves campaign Characters for reassignment and removes private Characters; it fences an unfinished group instead of completing a stale departure.
- **Joining or moving.** The owner can move the same Character into a campaign they can currently access; it is never copied and belongs to at most one campaign.
  - It doesn't put the Character on the militia roster. That stays a "Correct roster" Militia Correction.
  - "Add to a campaign" offers the active organization's campaigns.
- **Leaving a campaign.** The owner can take a Character out, back to no campaign or into another accessible campaign. Every actual departure, including automatic departures below, applies these changes together:
  - It takes the Character off the militia roster, out of its officer roles and out of team management. This is recorded as a Militia Correction with an automatic reason, and Staged Action Choices it affects must be reviewed before Confirmation. The canonical departure helper also clears live item ownership, character action state and skill-benefit membership, plus Character targets, rewards, officer checks and overseers in carried events, Activity event candidates, event occurrences and Persistent Decisions. Affected events and decisions require explicit review before Confirmation; an existing acknowledgement cannot satisfy a newly invalidated review. Activity choices retain their review flag until replaced or reviewed. An unreferenced departure writes no Militia Correction and preserves both militia and draft revisions.
  - It detaches campaign homebrew while preserving the Character Sheet, under the contract in "Campaign homebrew moving with a Character" ([decision](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/244)).
  - A Militia-only Character becomes Full.
- **Losing organization access.** If the owner's account survives, all their Characters in that organization's campaigns automatically return to no campaign. No action by the departed owner is required. Revoked campaign access is enforced even while preservation cleanup retries; backend completion must not require the departed user's access.
  - **Prepared implementation (#706).** A signed `organizationMembership.deleted` delivery removes the membership and its directory row in one transaction, so every campaign read and write refuses the departed user at once. The same transaction records an `organizationDeparture` and schedules `organizationDepartures:drive` to start after a five-minute grace (`departureGraceMs`), so that an account deletion delivered alongside the removal takes precedence (#708); only the browser fixture's stand-in removal starts at once. Each driver step rechecks the account, the membership and each Character's owner, then advances one bounded stage of a trusted move to no campaign. That move is the #703 move: the same preparation, reconciliation, fencing and atomic publication, recorded with `departureId`. It runs on the owner's stored account rather than their identity, so it never needs the departed user's credentials, and its sheet change and automatic Militia Correction are attributed to the system actor `system:organizationDeparture`, not to the departed user. Publication applies the ordinary departure: Characters become Full, archived ones stay archived, campaign homebrew becomes the Character's own copies, and roster, officer and team-manager assignments are removed with an automatic Militia Correction. A failed step rolls back alone and is recorded on its Character, which is retried with backoff. After three failures that Character is set aside and retried hourly while the owner's other Characters continue; the departure finishes once every Character is returned or set aside. Maintenance and sheet authority hold the work without counting failures.
  - **Rechecks.** A removal's provider time fences older membership events, so a delayed `created` or `updated` cannot restore access, and a repeated removal changes nothing. Rejoining stops the return: Characters already returned stay private and the rest stay in the campaign. A member who takes ownership during the return fences that Character's move, as any reassignment does (`ownershipChanged`), so it stays in the campaign. A later removal after a rejoin starts a new round, which only moves Characters the owner then has in the organization. If the account no longer exists, the departure ends as `accountDeleted` and leaves account-deletion policy (#708) in charge. A voluntary move in progress when access was lost is replaced by the trusted return.
  - **Scope.** Only prepared sheets in fixture campaigns return. Characters in production campaigns keep legacy authority until activation (#413): they stay in the campaign, remaining members keep editing them, and the departure records how many were left as `legacyRetained`. A returned Character keeps its prepared-sheet flag, as on the voluntary move path, so its surviving owner can edit it.
  - **What people see.** `character.listCampaignCharacters` marks rows whose owner's account survives without the organization's membership as `ownerDeparted`. `organizationDepartures:listReturning` tells only the departed user which organizations still have Characters returning to them, and how many were set aside, without names or campaigns; the Characters area shows one line per organization. `characterMoves:status` reports a completed return with `origin: 'organizationDeparture'`. Historical links from frozen records open a returned Character exactly as they would a missing one.
- **Deleting an account.** Its owned Characters in campaigns stay there, editable by the remaining members, and visibly need an owner. Any current member can claim them or assign them to another current member under the same reassignment rules. Its owned Characters outside campaigns are deleted. `ownerId` is absent for the retained Characters until reassignment.
  - **Prepared implementation (#708).** A signed `user.deleted` delivery removes the account and its membership directory rows in one transaction, so every read and write refuses it at once, and records an `accountDeletion` by token. The same transaction schedules `accountDeletions:drive`, which handles the deleted owner's Characters one per step, each step rechecking that the deleted account still owns the Character. A Character in a prepared campaign stays where it is with `ownerId` removed and `ownerLastOperationId` set to the deletion's system operation; nothing else on it changes, so its sheet, own and campaign homebrew, Companion Relationships, accepted warnings and militia assignments are kept, and an unfinished move is fenced as `ownershipChanged`. A Character outside a campaign is deleted with its own definitions through the same helper as an owner's private deletion (#260); a Companion Relationship between two deleted Characters stays only as interrupted history. Failures, set-aside retries, maintenance and sheet authority behave as for a departure, recorded on `accountDeletionCharacter`.
  - **Precedence.** Provider user IDs are never reused, so a deletion is final and carries no provider time. Once it is known, every later or reordered `user.created`, `user.updated` and membership event for that account is acknowledged and changes nothing, so the provider stops retrying and nothing restores the account or its access. A membership removal for an account the app has not seen at all is still refused so the provider retries it after the account's creation arrives. A removal that arrives first revokes access at once, but its return waits for the grace. A deletion that arrives within the grace wins: the departure ends as `accountDeleted` without moving anything, and the Characters stay in the campaign, ownerless. A deletion after the grace finds the return under way or done: the departure ends as `accountDeleted`, Characters it hadn't yet returned stay in the campaign, ownerless, with their return fenced, and a Character it had already returned is private, so it is deleted as a genuine completed private departure. Rejoining within the grace ends the departure as `membershipRestored` with nothing moved. A member who takes or assigns ownership first makes later steps for that Character do nothing, so stale jobs cannot undo the reassignment.
  - **What people see.** A retained Character's owner projection is `null` in `character.listCampaignCharacters`, `character.listByCampaign` and the sheet read, with `ownerDeparted: false`. Where members can assign owners, the owner control shows "Needs an owner" (a badge in the campaign Characters list and Characters & officers) beside two actions: **Take ownership**, which assigns the viewer in one click, and **Assign owner**, whose picker lists only current members. The campaign Characters page adds one line above its table, such as "2 Characters need an owner.", while any do. A member watching when the owner disappears sees "This Character no longer has an owner." Taking ownership replaces that notice with the viewer's own "Owner assigned."; other members watching see "Owner changed by another player." Nobody can leave or move an ownerless Character until a member takes ownership.
  - **Scope.** Only Characters in fixture campaigns become ownerless, flat and prepared-sheet ones alike (`isOwnerAssignable`), because members can assign their owner there and a deleted account can't keep owning anything. A departure is deliberately narrower (`isReturnableSheet`): it must move the Character through the #703 machinery, which needs a prepared sheet, so a flat Character in a fixture campaign stays there with its departed owner. Either way the Character stays in its campaign. Characters in production campaigns keep their stored `ownerId` and legacy authority until activation (#413), stay editable by the remaining members and are counted as `legacyRetained`; their owner projection is already `null` because the account is gone. #413 must remove `ownerId` from every Character whose owner token has an `accountDeletion`, and resume any deletion held by sheet authority.
- **Deleting a campaign or organization through the app.** First return every Character with a surviving owner to no campaign; ownerless Characters must be assigned before deletion can proceed.
  - **Prepared implementation (#710): campaigns.** Any current member can ask with `campaignDeletions.request`. It is refused with a `DELETION_BLOCKED` error, carrying counts, while any Character in the campaign needs an owner: one without an owner, or whose owner's account is gone even before account-deletion work has run. It is also refused while the campaign holds a legacy flat Character, which can't move through the #703 machinery. Otherwise it records a `campaignDeletion` and schedules `campaignDeletions:drive`, which returns each Character to its current surviving owner, archived ones included, through the same move machinery as a departure, recorded with `campaignDeletionId`. The changes are attributed to `system:campaignDeletion`. Returns use the shared lifecycle driver's failure records, backoff and set-aside retries (`campaignDeletionCharacter`). The deletion waits for set-aside Characters, checking again hourly. Each step rechecks the deletion, the Character's campaign and its current owner. A member who takes ownership during the return gets the Character instead, so a stale return cannot undo a reassignment. A Character that loses its owner during the returns pauses the deletion as `blocked` with its counts; once members assign it, a new request starts a new round. The campaign stays in use while Characters return; it is no longer offered as a move destination, and a Character added meanwhile is returned too. Once the campaign holds no Characters, the driver deletes the campaign row in one transaction, so every campaign read and write refuses it from then on. It then clears the live data in bounded steps: weekly drafts with their operations, targets and reviews, source corrections, setup, militia state, campaign homebrew, spell membership and the militia. Resolution Records are kept. The `campaignDeletion` row stays as the tombstone: it keeps the campaign's name, organization and militia for history access, and its fixture identity for returned Characters' catalog isolation. A repeated request returns the deletion under way or done.
  - **Prepared implementation (#710): organizations.** An organization admin asks with `organizationDeletions.request`, because only an admin can then delete the organization with Clerk. It is refused while any Character in any of its campaigns needs an owner or can't be returned. That check reads at most 4096 Characters (one campaign's limit) across the organization's campaigns, so the request stays within transaction read limits. A larger organization starts its deletion unchecked: each campaign deletion still refuses to delete a campaign while a Character needs an owner, and pauses the organization's deletion as `blocked`. It starts each campaign's deletion; a campaign that blocks pauses the organization's deletion as `blocked`, and asking again after assignment restarts the blocked campaigns. New campaigns are refused while the deletion is under way. Once every campaign is deleted the deletion is `ready`, and the admin deletes the organization with Clerk. The provider event that follows completes it as an external deletion that finds nothing left to keep.
  - **Owner decisions (2026-10-07).** Any campaign member may delete a campaign; organization deletion stays admin-only, as Clerk requires. A deletion can't be cancelled once started. Clerk's own Delete organization button in the organization profile stays enabled: a deletion made there is an external deletion, the tested path below. Legacy flat Characters block deletion until #413, set-aside Characters hold a deletion open with an hourly recheck, and deleted-campaign history and tombstones are kept indefinitely.
  - **Departures and deletions together.** A departure (#706) and a campaign deletion can both return the same Character. Each continues the other's unfinished return for the same owner instead of replacing it, so the Character is returned once.
- **An organization deleted externally.** Deletion cannot wait for assignment. Return Characters with surviving owners to no campaign; preserve ownerless Characters, inaccessible to users, for manual recovery by the app operator. This is an operational action, not a gameplay role or a right for former members to claim Characters. Preserve all sheet dependencies needed for recovery. A normal Character outside a campaign must have a surviving owner; these ownerless records are retained recovery data, not browseable private Characters.
  - **Prepared implementation (#710).** A signed `organization.deleted` delivery reaches `organizationDeletions:recordProviderDeletion`, which records the deletion on the organization's `organizationDeletion` row with `deletedAt`. Every organization access check reads it (`hasAccessToOrg`, `requireOrganizationMembership`, `user.getOrgAccessStatus` and move destinations), so every member's access ends in that transaction, before any membership row changes. Provider organization IDs are never reused, so the deletion is final. A repeated deletion and every later or reordered membership event for the organization are acknowledged and change nothing, so no event restores access or starts another return. `organizationDeletions:drive` then removes the memberships listed in the membership directory, a few per step, through the #706 departure helper. Each surviving owner's Characters return through an ordinary departure after its grace, so a known account deletion still takes precedence (#708). A departure already under way continues. Once no departure from the organization is still returning, the driver deletes each prepared campaign left without Characters, as a campaign deletion does, keeping its Resolution Records. A campaign that still holds Characters (ownerless ones, or ones whose return keeps failing) is kept intact with its sheets, homebrew and militia data as operator recovery data, and counted in `recoveryRetained`. A campaign whose own deletion was under way finishes it, and is counted in `recoveryRetained` while it is blocked or holds Characters whose return keeps failing. A surviving owner's Character whose return keeps failing in both the departure and the campaign deletion therefore stays in the inaccessible campaign: its owner can't reach it until a later retry returns it, and the operator sees it in `listRecovery`. The owner has accepted this. By the owner's decision (2026-10-07), a kept campaign stays whole, with its militia, drafts and catalog, as operator recovery data; a purge policy comes with #413. `organizationDeletions:listRecovery` is an internal query that lists the kept Characters for the operator; no user, former member included, can reach them.
  - **Scope.** Production campaigns keep legacy authority until activation (#413): they can't be deleted through the app, and after an external deletion they are kept intact, counted in `legacyRetained`, while their Characters stay with their stored owners. The organization deletion removes only memberships in the membership directory. A production member missing from the directory until its backfill completes keeps the deleted organization in `user.orgIds`, but the deletion record still refuses their access. #413 must run the directory backfill before activation; delete or keep, by policy, the campaigns an organization deletion kept as `legacyRetained`; and offer app deletion for activated campaigns.
- **Lifecycle processing.** Handlers must check current authoritative account, membership and ownership state and be safe to retry. A known deleted account follows account-deletion policy, not surviving-account membership-loss policy. A real earlier departure that already made a Character private still follows private-Character deletion if its owner later deletes their account. Preserve source data and dependencies until departure detachment or recovery completes successfully.
- **History.** Frozen Resolution Records, including superseded audit records, remain unchanged through departure or deletion. A deleted campaign's history remains available read-only to current members of its surviving organization; a deleted organization's history is inaccessible to users. Historical references never grant access to a current private Character Sheet.
  - **Prepared implementation (#710).** `canonicalHistory.list` and `canonicalHistory.read` resolve a deleted campaign through its `campaignDeletion` tombstone and the militia it kept, then require current membership of the organization, which refuses everyone once the organization is deleted. Membership is checked before anything else, for live and deleted campaigns alike: an outsider gets the same "Campaign access required" refusal for a campaign with history, one without, a deleted one and one that never existed. Only a member learns that a campaign has no history. Every write path refuses the deleted campaign because its campaign and militia rows are gone. `campaignDeletions.list` pages through the organization's deleted campaigns for its current members, with `hasHistory`, so their finished weeks stay reachable; a campaign still returning its Characters stays in the live campaign list. `campaignDeletions.status` reports one campaign's deletion: its state, Characters remaining, set-aside returns and blockers. Both return nothing to anyone without access. A Character named in the history opens only for its current owner, as any private Character does.
- **Deleting.** The owner can delete a Character in no campaign. Inside a campaign, a Character can only be archived (`isActive: false`), as today.
- **Catalog outside a campaign.** A Character in no campaign uses the global catalog and its own character-scoped entries. "Save to catalog" needs a campaign. Character-scoped entries stay with the Character when it joins or leaves.
- **App shell:**
  - **Top-level areas.** Campaigns (filtered to the active organization) and Characters (every Character you own, across organizations, grouped "No campaign" first and then by campaign).
  - **Inside a campaign.** The top bar adds the campaign-level pages Home, Characters and Militia.
  - **Inside Militia.** A left rail holds Week N · Finished weeks · Militia · Characters & officers, plus Setup until the militia is set up. On phone, the bottom bar has fixed tabs (Campaign · Militia · Characters · More), and a strip under the top bar holds the current tab's pages. The approved layout is [Prototype the app shell with Campaigns and Characters areas](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/213).
  - **Sheets.** A sheet keeps the campaign's top bar when its Character is in one, and opens with only a back button to the page it came from. Opening a Character in another organization's campaign switches the active organization.
- **Where Characters are created:**
  - the Characters area (no campaign, Full);
  - a campaign's Characters page (Full, and **Add from my characters**);
  - Characters & officers (Militia-only);
  - **Add to campaign** on a sheet in no campaign.
