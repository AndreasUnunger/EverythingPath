## Attacks

Decided by [Prototype attacks and conditional modifiers on the living sheet](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/216) (variant 3, tag `prototype-approved/attacks-conditionals`), [Decide which attack options an Attack Routine supports](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/229) and [Decide how the builder treats the remaining CRB attack feats and open attack rulings](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/237). Its rules follow the CRB. Foundry stores none of them as data, so they are written from the text. [Collect the official rules for attack options, natural attacks and flurry](https://github.com/AndreasUnunger/EverythingPath/issues/235) (`research/pf1-attack-rules`) collected that text from the CRB and FAQ, flagged the Bestiary's, and swept the CRB feat chapter for anything missed. The rules here are final, and its open items are cited as A1–A15.

- **Coverage.** The builder writes by hand every CRB feat, class feature and Combat situation that changes a routine line's bonus, damage, critical or number of attacks. Other books are catalog content: computed when a plain Modifier with its condition expresses it, and text otherwise. Text:
  - feats that change no line's numbers: Cleave, Great Cleave, Spring Attack, Whirlwind Attack, Shot on the Run, Ride-By Attack, Unseat, Trample, Stunning Fist, Scorpion Style, Gorgon's Fist, Shatter Defenses, Channel Smite, Penetrating Strike, Greater Penetrating Strike, Shield Slam, Combat Reflexes, Strike Back and the maneuver feats;
  - multipliers: Spirited Charge and Deadly Stroke;
  - what the sheet doesn't compute: Far Shot's adjustment to range increments, mounted penalties (Mounted Archery) and the target's AC (Improved Precise Shot, Pinpoint Targeting);
  - the eight critical feats, such as Bleeding Critical, and Critical Mastery;
  - feats changing improvised attacks: Catch Off-Guard, Improvised Weapon Mastery and Throw Anything;
  - Improved Natural Attack, which isn't CRB and which no Modifier expresses.
- **Attack Routines.**
  - A Character attacks through Attack Routines, state-only sheet entries. Each names the main weapon and whether it is held in two hands or one, an optional off-hand weapon, its natural attacks, and the Routine Options switched on.
  - Adding a weapon to Gear also adds a routine for it, held its natural way. A shield doesn't: its bash is picked into a routine by hand. The player renames, edits, deletes and adds routines.
  - A routine whose weapon has left Gear stays, with an advisory warning.
  - **Prepared manufactured-weapon slices (#314, #321).** `attackRoutine` state stores its name, main `weaponEntryId`, `hands` (`one`/`two`), and `mode` (`melee`/`ranged`/`thrown`), plus optional discriminated `offHand: { kind: 'weapon', weaponEntryId, mode } | { kind: 'otherEnd', mode }`. The weapon variant references a Character Sheet Entry ID; the other-end variant names the main weapon's second end; passing `offHand: null` to a writer clears the choice. Its state-only entry has no copied weapon definition. Adding a manufactured weapon through either writer atomically creates its default named routine; armor and shields do not. The same hands/mode defaults apply when replacing the main weapon, with explicitly supplied choices taking precedence and the off-hand choice retained. The public calculator returns separate single/full lines with BAB iteratives, current ability modifiers, size, weapon enhancement/masterwork, proficiency/armor penalties, Strength damage, critical and range breakdowns. Full attacks append the off-hand chain after the main-hand iteratives; each line identifies its `hand` (`main`/`off`) and `end` (`primary`/`otherEnd`). Active ordinary feat Selections supply Two-Weapon Fighting, Improved/Greater Two-Weapon Fighting and Double Slice by durable rule identity. Their combat feat types and typed prerequisites use the #313 selection model; prerequisite mismatches warn without disabling the choices. Greater Two-Weapon Fighting without Improved retains only its own extra attack, with the missing prerequisite warning. Two-Weapon Fighting penalty contributions credit its Selection; otherwise they credit the routine. The calculated routine supplies a two-weapon penalty summary when both weapons can be calculated. Single attacks keep the recorded main-hand configuration and omit two-weapon penalties and off-hand extras. Ability contributions identify their ability, iterative penalties identify BAB, and weapon facts identify the Gear entry. Haste, the remaining automatic feats and Routine Options, Power Attack included, are #407's (below). Ranged and thrown rate calculation is implemented by #322 below.
  - **Prepared ranged and thrown state (#322).** Routines additionally retain optional `ammunitionEntryId` and `rangeIncrements` (a safe positive integer, default 1; writes refuse more than 10, or 5 when either hand throws). There is no stored free-hand choice: whether the other hand can reload is derived from the routine's off hand, its hands and an active non-buckler shield in Equipment. Passing `ammunitionEntryId: null` clears the choice. Ammunition is a Gear item whose definition has `detail.ammunition: true`; adding ammunition without weapon statistics (arrows/bolts) creates no default Attack Routine. Shuriken retain weapon statistics and get their normal default thrown routine. Main/off-hand weapon choices identify the weapons already held, without a separate held-count input. Runtime weapon facts may include `reload: 'free' | 'move' | 'fullRound'`. Non-free crossbow reload, or a free reload without a free other hand, permits only one shot per hand in a full attack. A matching active Rapid Reload Selection, chosen by crossbow `baseType` in `state.choice` and matched against the typed crossbow list ignoring case and separators, changes hand/light reload to free and heavy reload to move. Quick Draw restores thrown iteratives whatever the other hand holds, and shuriken are drawn as ammunition. Without the required draw/reload rule or free hand, additional attacks for the affected hand are omitted; one already-held attack remains. Main-hand thrown Strength is ×1 even with two hands; off-hand Strength follows the existing ×½/Double Slice rules. Composite-bow rating caps and the −2 attack penalty below rating remain rules-driven.
  - **Ranged identities, Situations and feedback (#322).** Each attack line retains `weaponEntryId`, nullable `launcherEntryId`, nullable `ammunitionEntryId` (the routine's choice on every ranged line, main or off hand) and nullable `rangePenalty`, in both single/full sequences. Missing, unavailable, switched-off or non-ammunition Gear choices remain recorded with routine-owned advisory warnings while launcher lines remain calculable. Enhancement/Item Ability combination belongs to #408; recording ammunition never consumes its quantity. Range applies −2 per increment after the first; a recorded count beyond five thrown or ten projectile increments (after a later mode change) warns once per routine without blocking it. Far Shot remains text-only. The resolver supplies `attackRateNotes` for reload/draw conditions. Point-Blank Shot is an ordinary conditional feat Modifier in the shared `within30ft` Situation, labeled “within 30 ft.”; shooting into melee is the built-in `shootingIntoMelee` Combat situation, removed numerically by active Precise Shot. These are independent, local previews using the existing Situation identity and fresh-calculation mechanism. The form retains empty/malformed/over-maximum range drafts with distinct field errors, parses only valid decimal whole counts for mutations, and saves ammunition choices independently. New ammunition choices identify a Gear item; unrelated writes and unchanged choices preserve retained missing or non-item references for repair. The [calculation contract](../attack-calculation.md) records rules, sources and tested controller/view-model seams.
  - **Prepared natural/unarmed slice (#323).** Natural attacks use ordinary `item` entries and `weapon.naturalAttack: { type, role? }`, retaining their weapon `baseType` for feat choices. Twelve generic types receive Bestiary p. 301 size/default-role facts (explicitly non-CRB). Additional `naturalAttackEntryIds` reference same-Character natural entries; each claw is distinct. Missing or inactive choices retain routine-owned warnings, including when the main weapon is unavailable. The built-in literal `weaponEntryId: 'unarmed'` creates no Gear record and is also valid as an off-hand weapon. Natural and unarmed attacks are always proficient. Each calculated line adds `attackKind`, optional `naturalRole` and unarmed `damageKind` (`nonlethal` or `lethalOrNonlethal`). A sole possessed natural attack receives 1½ positive Strength; possessing more but choosing only one does not. A sole possessed natural form with several entries becomes primary. Mixed full attacks make all natural lines secondary without adding two-weapon penalties to the lone weapon; Multiattack changes only the secondary penalty to −2. The single result retains the main's standalone role. Natural lines have no iteratives. Optional `availableLimbs` defaults to two before weapon occupancy; excess claw/slam/tentacle attacks warn without blocking. The pure calculator accepts source-backed `monkUnarmedStrike: { dice, size?, sheetEntryId }` and familiar `naturalAttackAbility: 'greaterStrengthDexterity'` options. The companion loader's familiar projection supplies the familiar option; monk-level automation remains later work. Unknown damage scaling or a printed Fine-size dash produces no guessed line. Player-added racial/companion natural entries require no new entry kind; virtual Grant references remain later work. See [the calculation contract](../attack-calculation.md). These prepared entries and Selections are not a curated Catalog Release; no production migration or cutover is performed.
  - **Prepared Routine Options and automatic feats (#407).** `attackRoutine` state optionally records `options`: switched-on feat Catalog Entry IDs, saved as a whole list by `createAttackRoutine` and `editAttackRoutine` (an empty list clears them). New choices must be accessible feat definitions that are Routine Options; a retained choice survives its feat leaving, with a routine-owned `missingRoutineOption` advisory, and has no effect. A feat counts as a Routine Option when its rule identity is one of the hand-written CRB options or one of its Modifiers carries `condition.option`; no catalog flag is stored. Options match active Selections by Catalog Entry or, for copies, rule identity. The representative selection catalog adds Deadly Aim, Combat Expertise, Arcane Strike, Rapid Shot, Manyshot, Vital Strike and its upgrades, Lunge, Medusa's Wrath, Improved Critical and Two-Weapon Rend, with Power Attack's CMB penalty as data. The calculated routine adds `options`, `optionNotes` and `twoWeaponRend`; lines add `extraAttack`, `projectiles` and `vitalStrike`. Haste equivalence, better-ability Weapon Finesse, single threat doubling and Two-Weapon Rend apply automatically. The [calculation contract](../attack-calculation.md) records each rule, source and fixture outcome. These prepared Selections are not a curated Catalog Release; no production migration or cutover is performed.
  - **Prepared flurry of blows (#414).** The monk's and the unchained monk's Flurry of Blows class features are Routine Options, recorded in `options` by their Catalog Entry IDs and offered while their Grant counts. The calculated routine adds `flurry` (version, class feature, own version's monk level, flurry base attack bonus breakdown, extra attacks and attack penalty); flurry extra attacks carry `extraAttack.kind: 'flurry'`. `flurryNaturalAttacks`, `flurryWeapon` and `flurryArmor` are routine-owned advisories, and a recorded flurry whose Grant no longer counts warns `missingRoutineOption`. Weapon definitions gain the curated `monk` fact (the CRB p. 144 quality); the representative Base Items add the quarterstaff and kama. `switchClassVersion` maps a recorded flurry to the other version's. The [calculation contract](../attack-calculation.md) records each rule, source and fixture outcome. These prepared definitions are not a curated Catalog Release; no production migration or cutover is performed.
  - **Prepared double-end state (#321).** Runtime weapon definitions may include `otherEnd: { dice, threat, mult, damageTypes? }`; item state independently records `otherEnd: { masterwork?, enhancement?, material? }`. The primary end keeps the item's existing fields. `editEquipment` accepts `end: 'primary' | 'otherEnd'`, defaulting to the primary end, and patches only the supplied fields of that end. The routine controller exposes both end states and an end-bound save; the immediate end form keeps per-field feedback and refused or invalid drafts. Item Abilities and material mechanics remain their later item-state slices.
  - **Off-hand calculation and per-end state (#321).** Table 8–7 penalties apply only while the off hand can be calculated; missing or invalid off hands leave main attacks intact without those penalties. Double Slice restores full positive Strength to off-hand damage; negative Strength applies in full. Using both ends gives the primary full attack Str ×1; a single end held in two hands retains Str ×1½. The weapon definition's optional `otherEnd` stores `dice`, `threat`, `mult` and optional `damageTypes` (otherwise inherited). Item state stores independent `otherEnd: { masterwork?, enhancement?, material? }`. The equipment writer's optional `end` defaults to `primary`, or selects `otherEnd`, and patches only that end. Separate off-hand weapons reference sheet-row IDs, so no Catalog Copy traversal changes are needed. The discriminated off-hand state replaces a sentinel shape introduced only on this unactivated branch; no production backfill or new Convex module is needed. Representative gnome hooked hammer and four attack feats support isolated prepared demonstrations; they are not a curated Catalog Release. No deployment or production cutover is performed by this slice. The [calculation contract](../attack-calculation.md) records canonical feat identities and the deferred Power Attack seam.
  - **Warnings and repair.** Missing Gear, switched-off Gear, a weapon whose source is unavailable, and a selected entry that is no longer a weapon produce distinct warnings owned by the routine, with no guessed attack lines. A same-Character non-item reference does not prevent the sheet from loading; a reference to another Character still fails authorization. Routines keep their name and recorded choices and remain available for rename, removal or replacement. Unsuitable hands/mode and one-handed exotic use are advisory warnings shown both on the card and in the editor, including when attacks can be calculated. Throwing a weapon not designed for throwing applies the CRB's −4 improvised penalty and 10-foot range increment (CRB pp. 141–142); selecting an unusual configuration remains allowed. Off-hand warnings remain evaluated when the main weapon is switched off or missing, so an unchanged Accepted Warning survives unrelated writes. Off-hand warning copy names its weapon and says only off-hand attacks are skipped. Proficiency-use facts carry the real routine ID and explicit hand. Switched-off, missing and unavailable weapons supply no proficiency-use facts.
  - **Editing and collaboration.** Changes persist immediately, field by field. Any authorized player may edit a campaign routine, and the latest supplied change wins; no expected revision or stale-save refusal is used. The server returns an acknowledged routine revision solely for local draft bookkeeping. Incoming changes refresh untouched fields while retaining dirty or invalid drafts, with “Changed by another player. Your edits are kept.” Saving, saved, error and retry feedback stays beside the affected control; a failed save preserves the draft. Routine names and main weapon IDs remain required; an absent off hand, a missing referenced Gear row, and rule-invalid hand combinations remain saveable with advisory warnings. Other structurally invalid values are blocked with field errors. The editor's preview reads each calculated line's own weapon, damage and critical values, including distinct double ends. Internal identifiers and revisions are not player-facing copy.
  - **Removal and Undo.** `active: false` is the only removal state. Removal retains the same row and choices, omits its calculated attacks, and prunes its Accepted Warnings. A Character retains at most one removed routine for Undo: removing another routine permanently purges the earlier removed row and its warning acceptances. Routine writes, and writers that automatically create routines, retire accumulated historical removals before checking sheet size. Undo restores the retained row's original ID; an earlier purged removal cannot be restored. Only an acknowledged removal creates a local Undo receipt. Failed Undo keeps that receipt available for retry; removal and restoration notices can be dismissed. This does not copy or re-add Gear.
  - **Prepared schema transition.** The earlier prepared representation additionally stored `state.deleted: true`. This schema removes that optional field and adds `by_characterId_and_kind_and_active`. Before deploying over such prepared data, remove `state.deleted` from attack-routine states while retaining `active: false`, the latest Undo row's ID and choices, and remove older inactive routines plus their warning acceptances. Ordinary writes clean up historical inactive rows within a transaction; data exceeding Convex transaction limits needs operator cleanup in bounded batches before deployment. No production migration or deployment is performed by #314. The generated API declaration includes `lib/representativeWeaponCatalog` and must be verified by real codegen when deployment is authorized.
  - Runtime weapon facts retain the imported vocabulary `handedness`, `dice`, `damageTypes`, `threat`, `mult`, `thrown`, `finesse` and `rangeIncrement`. The #306 runtime proficiency adapter keeps singular `baseType`; `attackType`, `thrownRangeIncrement`, `strengthDamage` and `strengthRating` make the selected mode and ranged Strength rule explicit. The representative prepared Base Items cite CRB Table 6–4 and weapon descriptions and carry Medium-size printed dice; they are not a curated Catalog Release. Import admission continues to use its broader plural `baseTypes` shape, so a later importer/runtime adapter must map that shape explicitly.
- **Weapons.**
  - **Unarmed strike** is a built-in weapon every sheet can put in a routine, with no Gear item. It is imported from Foundry's `monster-abilities` unarmed strike (1d3 nonlethal). Its lines say "nonlethal", or "lethal or nonlethal" with Improved Unarmed Strike. The −4 penalty for dealing the other kind of damage stays text. A monk's unarmed damage scales with monk level. For sizes other than Small, Medium and Large, unarmed and monk unarmed damage step from the Medium value along the CRB FAQ damage-dice chart (March 2015) (A14).
  - **Natural attacks:** primary at full BAB with Str, ×1.5 if it is the only natural attack; secondary at −5 with ½ Str. They get no iterative attacks. Foundry's 12 generic natural attack items (Bite, Claw… in `monster-abilities`, `attack` items with `subType: natural`) import as natural weapons. Their primary or secondary type is written from the rules, because Foundry marks it on only 5. Nothing grants them, because racial natural-weapon traits are prose in Foundry: the player adds a natural attack entry. Grants can come later without a model change.
  - **Natural attacks with weapons** (A5; CRB p. 182, the Bestiary's universal monster rule, the UM FAQ synthesist and tentacle entries):
    - A routine may mix them. All its natural attacks are then secondary: −5 and ½ Str, −2 with Multiattack.
    - The weapon attacks take no two-weapon penalty from the natural attacks. Table 8-7 applies only with an off-hand weapon, and Two-Weapon Fighting doesn't change natural attacks.
    - A routine whose claws, slams or tentacles outnumber its limbs not holding a weapon shows an advisory warning.
    - A flurry routine can't include natural attacks.
  - **Thrown weapons:** a weapon with a thrown attack has a melee or thrown mode in a routine. Foundry stores separate `mwak` and `twak` actions on one item (dagger, spear, javelin). Thrown takes Dex to attack and Str ×1 to damage however many hands throw it, because the ×1½ rule says "melee attacks"; an off-hand throw takes ×½ (A9).
    - **Rate (A9).** Without Quick Draw a thrown routine gets one throw per hand already holding a weapon: no iterative, haste or extra off-hand attacks, as with a crossbow that doesn't reload free. Quick Draw restores the full rate. Shuriken are exempt, because they are drawn as ammunition.
  - **Double weapons:** the off hand may be the other end of the main weapon, light for two-weapon penalties. Foundry models only one end for 16 of 17 double weapons, so the Curation Overlay supplies the second end's dice and critical. Each end has its own enchantment (see "Weapons and armor").
    - Used as a double weapon, the primary end takes Str ×1 and the off-hand end ×½ (#321), with plain Power Attack on the primary end and half on the off-hand end (#407, A10). Str ×1½ and Power Attack +50% apply when wielding it two-handed to attack with one end.
  - **Weapon choices.** A natural attack form (Bite, Claw…) is a valid Weapon Focus, Weapon Specialization and Improved Critical choice, matched on `baseType` like a weapon (A8).
- **Single and full attack.**
  - Each routine resolves to its single attack (a standard action: the main weapon, no two-weapon penalty, no extra attacks) and its full attack in order.
  - **Iterative attacks:** one more at −5 cumulative at BAB +6, +11 and +16.
  - **Off hand:** one off-hand attack.
  - **Haste:** an active `haste` Source adds one attack at the highest bonus.
  - **Reload:** a crossbow gets no iterative or haste attacks unless reloading is a free action. Thrown weapons without Quick Draw follow the same rule (see "Weapons").
- **Attack bonus:**
  - BAB, plus Str for melee or Dex for ranged;
  - the `attack.*` Modifiers and the weapon's own conditional Modifiers;
  - the item's enhancement and masterwork, built-in Modifiers with `weapon: '$self'` (see "Weapons and armor");
  - two-weapon penalties (CRB Table 8-7): −6/−10, −4/−8 with a light off-hand weapon, −4/−4 with Two-Weapon Fighting, −2/−2 with both;
  - the switched-on Routine Options;
  - −2 with a composite bow whose Strength rating exceeds the Str bonus;
  - nonproficiency penalties (see "Proficiencies").
- **Damage:**
  - the weapon's dice;
  - Str ×1.5 in two hands, ×1 in one, ×0.5 off hand (a Str penalty applies in full); Str up to its rating for a composite bow, no Str for a crossbow, a Str penalty only for other bows;
  - the `damage.*` and weapon Modifiers, the item's enhancement included (masterwork adds nothing to damage).
  - **Sneak attack:** +1d6 per entry, as conditional damage in its own Situation (flanking or the target denied its Dex bonus).
  - **Special-ability dice:** always-on dice are an extra damage part ("1d8+4 plus 1d6 fire"). Target-dependent ones (_holy_, _bane_) are Situational damage, like sneak attack. They come from Item Abilities (see "Item Abilities").
- **Critical:** the weapon's threat range and multiplier, shown as "×3", "19–20/×2" or "18–20/×2".
- **Automatic feats** apply with no toggle while on the sheet:
  - Improved and Greater Two-Weapon Fighting: a second and third off-hand attack, at −5 and −10;
  - Double Slice: full Str on the off hand;
  - Threat-range expanders: Improved Critical, _keen_ and _keen edge_ (`doublesThreat`). Any number of them double the threat range once. This follows AoN's later CRB printing of Improved Critical (A15), and is a hand-written rule, as no Modifier target is a threat range;
  - Rapid Reload: a light crossbow's reload becomes free and a heavy crossbow's a move action;
  - Quick Draw: the full rate for thrown routines (see "Weapons");
  - Weapon Finesse: the higher of Str and Dex to attack with a `finesse` weapon. The breakdown names the ability used, and a shield's armor check penalty applies;
  - Multiattack: secondary natural attacks take −2 instead of −5. It is a Bestiary feat, written by hand because the CRB natural-attack rule names it;
  - Two-Weapon Rend: a routine with an off-hand weapon shows one extra line after its full attack, "Rend: 1d10 + 1½ Str, once per round if both hands hit". It is never added to a single attack line;
  - Shield Master: a shield bash in a routine takes no two-weapon penalty, because the FAQ limits its "any penalties" to two-weapon ones. The shield's enhancement also applies to its bash attack and damage "as if it was a weapon enhancement bonus", so it doesn't stack with the `bash` enchantment's own (see "Armor and shields").
- **Data-only feats.** Point-Blank Shot is a Curation Overlay Situational Modifier: +1 on ranged attack and damage in the shared Situation `within30ft` ("within 30 ft"). Critical Focus is a Situational Note with no target, shown with its entry, like any note on critical confirmation (see "Situational notes").
- **Combat situations** are built-in Situations: fighting defensively, total defense, charging, and shooting into melee (−4 on ranged attacks, CRB p. 182). Precise Shot removes the shooting-into-melee penalty, a hand-written rule. Combat situations don't trigger the situational marker. Each breakdown lists them in a collapsed "Combat situations" group below the Character's own "Only when…" groups.
  - Fighting defensively applies to routine lines. The sheet has no attack-of-opportunity line, so A11 needs no ruling.
- **On the sheet:**
  - The Offense block keeps BAB, CMB and Initiative. A separate Attacks block lists the routines as cards: the single attack, then the numbered full attack. Each line shows the weapon, its bonus, damage, critical and range, plus options as chips and penalties in one line.
  - Routines are edited in a side panel, or a bottom sheet on phone. Changes apply at once, and a delete can be undone.
  - **Situational bonuses never appear on the face of the sheet.** A small marker flags any number that has them: in the pinned stats, the Defenses, the skills and the attacks.
  - The number's breakdown has an "Only when…" section grouped by Situation. Each group shows its lines and what the total becomes then, with suppressed lines struck through. A while-active line that is waiting is dimmed ("only while raging").

### Routine Options

- **Data.** No catalog flag marks a Routine Option; it is derived. A feat Catalog Entry is a Routine Option when its durable `ruleIdentity` names one of the hand-written CRB rules (Power Attack, Rapid Shot, Manyshot, Vital Strike and its upgrades, Lunge, Medusa's Wrath, plus the data-only Deadly Aim, Combat Expertise and Arcane Strike) or when any of its Modifiers carries the `option` condition. Those Modifiers apply only inside a routine that has the option switched on; on targets outside its lines they become the option's Situation.
- **Offered.** A routine offers an option only while its entry is on the sheet. If the entry leaves, the routine keeps it switched on, with an advisory warning.
- **Outside the routine.** An option's Modifiers on anything but its routine's lines, such as Combat Expertise's dodge AC or Lunge's −2 AC, belong to a Situation named after the option. The AC breakdown shows "Only when using Combat Expertise".
- **CRB Routine Options:** Power Attack, Deadly Aim, Combat Expertise, Arcane Strike, Rapid Shot, Manyshot, Vital Strike (with Improved and Greater), Lunge and Medusa's Wrath. Deadly Aim, Combat Expertise and Arcane Strike are data only. Arcane Strike's damage reaches natural attacks and unarmed strikes too: the CRB has a Natural weapon group, and unarmed damage "is considered weapon damage" (A7). The others add hand-written rules:
  - **Power Attack:** −1 attack and +2 damage, plus −1 and +2 more per 4 BAB from +4. The damage is ×1.5 two-handed and ×0.5 off hand, and it applies to melee only.
  - **Rapid Shot:** one extra attack, and −2 on every attack.
  - **Manyshot:** a second arrow on the first attack.
  - **Vital Strike:** the weapon's dice ×2, ×3 with Improved and ×4 with Greater, on the single attack only. It applies to any attack with damage dice: natural attacks, unarmed strikes and monk unarmed damage (A6).
  - **Lunge:** +5 ft reach.
  - **Medusa's Wrath:** offered only on routines with an unarmed strike. It adds two more unarmed strikes at the highest bonus to the full attack. The player switches it on when the target qualifies.
- **Flurry of blows** is a Routine Option for monks, written separately for the core monk and the unchained monk.
  - **Weapons.** The routine's main weapon makes every flurry attack. If an off-hand weapon is set, it makes the flurry's extra attacks, laid out as two-weapon fighting. Other mixes are separate routines. Without an off-hand weapon, the extra attacks follow the iterative at their bonus, as Table: Monk prints them. A flurry routine can't include natural attacks: they stay recorded with a warning and leave the flurry, and a natural main weapon leaves the flurry without effect. Only unarmed strikes and weapons with the monk quality (CRB p. 144, the weapon fact `monk`) may flurry; another weapon keeps its flurry lines with a warning.
  - **Single and full.** The single attack never flurries. *Haste*'s extra attack stacks with the flurry's (FAQ qk0; PU p. 14).
  - **Loss.** Armor or a shield warns that the monk loses flurry, and the routine keeps its flurry lines. The sheet tracks no carried load, so a medium or heavy load can't warn.
  - **Versions.** Each flurry counts only its own version's monk levels (owner, #414). Switching the monk's class version maps a recorded flurry to the other version's; a flurry whose class feature no longer counts stays switched on with an advisory warning.
  - **Core flurry.** Every flurry attack takes full Str. Power Attack halves only the attacks made with the off-hand weapon; unarmed strikes and a single weapon have no off hand (A1). A two-handed monk weapon takes Str ×1, from the flurry's "full Strength bonus … with a weapon wielded in both hands", but Power Attack still gets +50% from its own two-handed clause (A2). The flurry is "as if using" Two-Weapon Fighting, not two-weapon fighting: the Two-Weapon Fighting chain adds no attacks on top, and Double Slice and Two-Weapon Rend don't apply (A3).
  - **Core base attack.** The monk class's base attack bonus becomes the monk level; other classes and racial Hit Dice add normally (FAQ naz). Power Attack and Combat Expertise use this flurry base attack bonus (FAQ o72), so Combat Expertise's dodge bonus to AC in a flurry routine equals its penalty there (owner, #414). The sheet's Combat Expertise AC Situation belongs to no routine and keeps the ordinary base attack bonus.
  - **Unchained flurry.** Unarmed strikes take full Str. Monk weapons follow the normal rules: one weapon in two hands takes Str ×1½ and Power Attack +50%, and off-hand weapon attacks take Str ×½ and half Power Attack. No two-weapon penalties apply (A4). The Two-Weapon Fighting chain adds no attacks, but Double Slice and Two-Weapon Rend apply to the off-hand lines (owner, #414).
  - **Not computed:** ki pool and style strikes, Rapid Shot with a shuriken flurry, and the zen archer's and monk of the empty hand's flurries stay text. The seeded Zen Archer replaces the monk's Flurry of Blows, so a recorded core flurry is dormant on a zen archer.
