# Prepared equipment and Proficiencies

Ticket #306 extends isolated prepared Character Sheets. Production keeps legacy authority and the three new writers use the legacy Character Write Gate, current membership/ownership access, campaign fixture or private demo gate, and warning pruning against the pending in-memory sheet. There is no production activation or migration.

`calculateCharacterSheet` derives `equipment`, `proficiencies`, `weaponProficiencies`, and `proficiencyPrerequisites`. Armor and shield base bonuses and their enhancement bonuses occupy their own AC leaves. Ordinary stacking selects the strongest bonus on each leaf; every active item's ACP and spell failure still contributes. The lowest armor/shield maximum Dexterity caps positive Dexterity to AC and touch AC, while CMD retains full Dexterity. Skills consume the same equipped-item facts once, independently of proficiency. Spell failure is displayed as a percentage, without casting simulation.

Armor detail uses the #253 normalized `bonus`, `maxDex`, `armorCheckPenalty`, `asf`, and `category` fields. Source categories `lightArmor`, `mediumArmor`, `heavyArmor`, `towerShield`, and shield `other` are accepted alongside the domain's light/medium/heavy/tower and named shield categories. `other` is the imported buckler category. Legacy ACP-only detail remains readable and does not invent nonproficiency attack penalties when the category is unknown.

`editEquipment` requires an item with armor or shield detail and accepts exactly one recorded `entryId` or durable `grantKey`, plus optional `active`, `enhancement`, `masterwork`, and nullable `material`. Materialized and synthesized Grants retain their identity, metadata and dormancy through edits. Enhancement implies masterwork; a state material applies the CRB adjustment once. Mithral adjusts armor and shields alike: ACP improves by 3, maximum Dexterity by 2, and spell failure by 10 percentage points. Specific catalog materials already include their material/masterwork adjustment. Other materials remain text. Enhancement above +5 is advisory; malformed enhancement or new armor numbers are refused. Existing malformed ACP/enhancement remains a sourced unresolved warning and omits the affected item's ACP.

Proficiencies derive from counting entries after the Grant Graph applies dormancy and replacements. Classes use durable class family identity and only their first Class Level supplies a choice. Category, named weapon, group, firearm and familiarity-as-martial grants are supported; always-proficient weapons remain proficient. Manual removals defeat every covering category, name or group grant. An empty choice grants nothing and appears in `missingChoices` without a warning. `setManualProficiency` atomically changes one manual addition/removal; `none` clears that explicit edit, preserving other players' edits. Class choice uses `editClassLevel`; recorded selections use `setProficiencyChoice`; Grants use `editGrantState`.

The calculator's `weaponUses: [{ entryId, hands, attack?: 'melee' | 'ranged' }]` option is the composition seam used by prepared Attack Routines. Every active routine supplies its selected main weapon, hands and melee/ranged use automatically. Ownership of a weapon alone never emits the one-handed exotic warning. Each use attributes its attack breakdown to the selected melee or ranged use (melee by default) and exposes weapon `attackPenalty`, `armorNonproficiencyPenalty`, `totalAttackPenalty`, and a combined breakdown with each source once. Nonproficient weapon attacks take −4; nonproficient armor/shields contribute their adjusted ACP. Bastard swords and dwarven waraxes in one hand require a grant naming their base type and otherwise raise the specified advisory warning. A named familiarity grant covers that use when martial training is present. The two-handed use accepts martial proficiency. Ticket #314 persists routines and supplies their weapon uses on sheet reads, so weapon nonproficiency −4 and the one-handed exotic warning now apply in prepared gameplay. Armor penalties already present in attack leaves are composed only once into each routine line.

Optional catalog `proficiencyPrerequisites` supports `{ kind: 'proficiency', proficiency: ManualProficiency | { choice: true } }`. Current checks use current grants/manual state; recorded checks rebuild the Grant Graph using preceding levels/ordered Selections, current race/equipment/effects/manual adjustments and current definitions. Prestige entry checks precede its first-level grants. Exempt feat slots skip checks. Unknown acquisition positions receive current checks only; unknown same-level order is not guessed. Missing named-weapon metadata and empty choices are left unmodeled. Failed checks remain advisory and use semantic fingerprints that exclude irrelevant grants and source document identities. New dated Selections default to the next add order; explicit recorded order can be supplied or cleared. Tickets #313 and #320 should fold recorded-level proficiency prerequisite reconstruction and `choiceOrder` into the general prerequisite engine.

The controllers and React Hook Form/Zod helpers provide equipment edits, manual proficiency choices, per-field save feedback, remote-change feedback and retained invalid/refused drafts.
