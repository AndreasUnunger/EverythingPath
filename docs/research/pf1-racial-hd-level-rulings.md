# Official rulings on racial Hit Dice and level-keyed rules (PF1)

Follow-up to `docs/research/pf1-racial-hit-dice.md` (branch `research/pf1-racial-hit-dice`). That note left five open items: 1, 3, 4 and 5, plus the ability score question. This note looks for official Paizo rulings on them, in this order of authority:

1. official FAQ;
2. errata;
3. rules text in a Paizo book, with the book named;
4. posts by Paizo staff on the paizo.com forums. These are **not official rules** and are listed separately.

Community opinions, d20pfsrd notes and PF2 material are left out. Everything was retrieved on 2026-10-02.

## Answer

| # | Question | Official FAQ / errata | Official rules text | Staff comment (unofficial) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Does "character level" count racial HD? | **None** | Indirect only. CRB: character level is the sum of class levels. Bestiary keys monster DCs to HD and gives monster cohorts a separate "cohort level". | None found |
| 2 | Feat count: total HD or character level? | **None** | **Total Hit Dice** (CRB Common Terms, Bestiary). Monster Codex stat blocks match. | None found for PF1 |
| 3 | Skill-rank cap = total HD? | **None** | **Yes**, stated outright in the CRB (Using Skills). Monster Codex stat blocks match. | None found |
| 4 | Saves and BAB when combining | **BAB: yes** (CRB FAQ, Monk). Saves: **none** | Saves: no explicit text. Monster Codex stat blocks add racial and class saves per source. | None found |
| 5 | Ability increases: HD or character level? | **None** | Bestiary: +1 per 4 **additional** HD when advancing a monster. CRB: character level 4, 8, 12, 16, 20. Nothing for the mixed case. | James Jacobs (2014): +1 per 4 *additional* HD, also when adding class levels. This reverses his own 2010 answer. |

Short version:

- **Q2, Q3 and the BAB half of Q4** are settled by official text, and Paizo's own stat blocks agree.
- **Q1, the saves half of Q4, and Q5** have no FAQ or errata. Only indirect rules text and one staff comment (Q5) bear on them.

## Q1. Does "character level" count racial Hit Dice?

**No official ruling exists.** No FAQ or errata addresses it.

### Rules text

- **Character level is defined by class levels.** CRB, Getting Started, Common Terms:
  > **Level:** A character's level represents his overall ability and power. There are three types of levels. Class level is the number of levels of a specific class possessed by a character. Character level is the sum of all of the levels possessed by a character in all of his classes.

  > **Monster:** Monsters are creatures that rely on racial Hit Dice instead of class levels for their powers and abilities (although some possess class levels as well).
- **The multiclassing sentence keeps the two terms apart, and it is the line community threads argue over.** CRB, Classes, Multiclassing:
  > Note that there are a number of effects and prerequisites that rely on a character's level or Hit Dice. Such effects are always based on the total number of levels or Hit Dice a character possesses, not just those from one class.

  It says to total whichever measure the effect uses. It does not say that one counts as the other.
- **The CRB treats "character level" and "HD" as different measures.** CRB, Wizard, Familiars:
  > **Hit Dice:** For the purpose of effects related to number of Hit Dice, use the master's character level or the familiar's normal HD total, whichever is higher.
- **Leadership is keyed to character level.** CRB, Feats, Leadership:
  > **Prerequisite:** Character level 7th.

  > **Leadership Score:** Your base Leadership score equals your level plus your Charisma modifier. … you must still be 7th level or higher in order to gain the Leadership feat.

  The feat's DCs, such as Stunning Fist, use "DC 10 + 1/2 your character level + your Wis modifier".
- **Monster abilities are keyed to Hit Dice instead.** Bestiary, Monster Creation:
  > The DC for almost all special abilities is equal to 10 + 1/2 the creature's Hit Dice + a relevant ability modifier

  One universal monster rule says "racial HD" explicitly. Universal Monster Rules, Stench: "DC 10 + 1/2 stench creature's racial HD + stench creature's Con modifier". Paizo wrote "HD" wherever it meant monster HD, and kept "character level" for feats.
- **A monster's "level" for Leadership is a separate, hand-assigned number.** Bestiary, Monster Cohorts:
  > With the GM's approval, this cohort can be a similarly aligned monster rather than a humanoid with the appropriate number of class levels. … their effective cohort "level" corresponds to the level available to the PC as afforded by his Leadership score.

  For example, a ghoul (2 HD, CR 1) is level 5, and a young dragon is "its CR + 8". This is neither racial HD nor CR.

  The Bestiary's [Monsters as PCs](https://legacy.aonprd.com/bestiary/monstersAsPCs.html) section is a third mapping: "Treat the monster's CR as its total class levels".

### Reading

The text supports "character level = class levels only". No text says racial HD satisfy a character-level prerequisite or a "1/2 your character level" DC, and none says they don't. Where Paizo needed a level-equivalent for a monster, it invented a separate number: cohort level, or CR as class levels. It never reused HD.

### Staff comment

None found from Sean K Reynolds, Jason Bulmahn, Mark Seifter or Stephen Radney-MacFarland. The main thread, [Do Racial Hit Dice count as Class Levels for feat prerequisites?](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2v3nw) (2018), has no staff post.

## Q2. Feat count: total Hit Dice or character level?

**No FAQ, but the official rules text says Hit Dice**, and Paizo's stat blocks agree.

### Rules text

- CRB, Getting Started, Common Terms:
  > **Feat:** … Creatures receive a number of feats based off their Hit Dice, but some classes and other abilities grant bonus feats.
- Bestiary, Monster Creation:
  > Each creature with an Intelligence score receives a number of feats equal to 1 + 1 per every 2 Hit Dice after the first (so, 1 at 1 HD, 2 at 3 HD, etc.).
- Bestiary, Monster Advancement, Adding Class Levels, Step 2:
  > Next, add the class levels to the monster, making all of the necessary additions to its HD, hit points, BAB, CMB, CMD, feats, skills, spells, and class features. … This functions just like adding class levels to a character without racial Hit Dice.
- The CRB's Table: Character Advancement and Level-Dependent Bonuses keys feats to "Character Level". That is the PC table. The glossary line above covers creatures in general.

### Monster Codex stat blocks (official, by example)

Feats = ceil(total HD / 2) plus class bonus feats. Character level would give one fewer in each case:

| Stat block | HD | Feats listed | By total HD | By class levels |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gnoll Sergeant (gnoll ranger 3) | 2 racial + 3 = 5 | 5 | 3 + Endurance + 1 combat style = **5** | 2 + 2 = 4 |
| Bugbear Stalker (bugbear ranger 3) | 3 racial + 3 = 6 | 5 | 3 + Endurance + 1 combat style = **5** | 2 + 2 = 4 |
| Ghoul Huntsmaster (ghoul ranger 6) | 2 racial + 6 = 8 | 7 | 4 + Endurance + 2 combat style = **7** | 3 + 3 = 6 |

### Staff comment

None for PF1. A 2006 James Jacobs post in [rzs2gyoq](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2gyoq) ("feats and skill points and ability increases are tied to Hit Dice") is about D&D 3.5 ECL and the Monster Manual. It is not PF1 and is left out.

## Q3. Maximum ranks per skill = total Hit Dice?

**No FAQ, but the official rules text is explicit: yes, total Hit Dice.**

### Rules text

- CRB, Using Skills:
  > You can never have more ranks in a skill than your total number of Hit Dice.
- CRB, Getting Started, Common Terms:
  > **Skill:** … As a creature gains Hit Dice, it also gains additional skill ranks that can be added to its skills.
- Bestiary, Monster Advancement, Step 4 multiplies ranks "times the total number of added Hit Dice". A higher Int modifier is applied "times its new total number of Hit Dice".

### Monster Codex stat blocks

- **Gnoll Sergeant (ranger 3, 5 HD).** Perception +9 = 5 ranks + 3 class skill + 1 Wis. That is 5 ranks with only 3 class levels.
- **Bugbear Stalker (ranger 3, 6 HD).** Perception +11 = 6 ranks + 3 + 2 Wis.

Both exceed character level, so the cap is total HD.

### Staff comment

None found.

## Q4. Saves and BAB when a creature with racial HD takes class levels

### BAB: official FAQ

CRB FAQ, "Monk" ([Core Rulebook FAQ](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm/)):

> A monk using flurry treats his BAB from monk levels as equal to his monk level. He still adds BAB from other sources (such as other classes or racial Hit Dice) normally to this total.

The Bestiary's Adding Class Levels step also lists "BAB" among the additions.

### Saves: no FAQ, no explicit text

The rules text is indirect:

- Bestiary, Adding Class Levels, Step 2 lists HD, hit points, BAB, CMB, CMD, feats, skills, spells and class features, but **not saves**. It ends "This functions just like adding class levels to a character without racial Hit Dice."
- CRB, Multiclassing:
  > He adds all of the hit points, base attack bonuses, and saving throw bonuses from a 1st-level wizard on top of those gained from being a 5th-level fighter.
- Bestiary, Adding Racial Hit Dice, Step 5: "adjust the creature's derived statistics, such as its initiative, AC, saving throws, … BAB".

### Monster Codex stat blocks (official, by example)

Base saves add per source: the racial progression and the class progression are computed separately and summed. A racial good save and a class good save therefore each start at +2. Pooling all HD into one progression would give lower numbers.

| Stat block | Racial base (HD, good saves) | Class base | Ability mods + items | Printed | Pooled alternative |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gnoll Sergeant | gnoll 2 HD, good Fort: F+3 R+0 W+0 | ranger 3: F+3 R+3 W+1 | Con +2, Dex +2, Wis +1, *cloak of resistance +1* | **Fort +9, Ref +6, Will +3** | 5-HD good Fort = +4, so Fort +7 |
| Bugbear Stalker | bugbear 3 HD, good Ref: F+1 R+3 W+1 | ranger 3: F+3 R+3 W+1 | Con +2, Dex +3, Wis +2 | **Fort +6, Ref +9, Will +4** | 6-HD good Ref = +5, so Ref +8 |
| Ghoul Huntsmaster | ghoul 2 HD (undead), good Will: F+0 R+0 W+3 | ranger 6: F+5 R+5 W+2 | Cha +4 (undead Fort), Dex +5, Wis +3 | **Fort +9, Ref +10, Will +8** | — |

BAB in the same blocks is also per source. The gnoll sergeant has +1 (2 HD, 3/4) + 3 (ranger) = **+4**. The bugbear stalker has +2 + 3 = **+5**.

### Staff comment

None found. [Do class and racial saving throw bonuses stack?](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2ql6d), [Adding Class Levels to Monster](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2q4ik) and [Racial hit die/base attack bonus](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2tlr3) have no staff posts on their first page.

## Q5. Ability score increases every 4: Hit Dice or character level?

**No FAQ, no errata, and no rules text for the mixed case.**

### Rules text

- **CRB.** Table: Character Advancement and Level-Dependent Bonuses gives "Ability Score" increases at character level 4th, 8th, 12th, 16th and 20th. The CRB FAQ entry "Ability Score Increases" says how much (+1) and to what, but not which level count.
- **Bestiary, Monster Advancement, Adding Racial Hit Dice, Step 3:**
  > For every 4 additional Hit Dice gained by the monster, add 1 to one of its ability scores.
- **Bestiary, Adding Class Levels.** It gives the +4/+4/+2/+2/+0/–2 adjustment for PC class levels and is silent on the every-4 increase.
- **Stat blocks don't settle it.** The Monster Codex blocks checked (gnoll sergeant, bugbear stalker, gnoll bruiser) don't reconcile cleanly with any rule; the array plus increases don't sum to the printed scores. James Jacobs says (below) that Paizo's adventures did it both ways.

### Staff comment (unofficial): James Jacobs, Creative Director

- **2014, the later post.** [Monsters with class levels and their ability score increases](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2qxww), 2014-04-29. The thread asked about **class levels added to a monster with racial HD**:
  > The correct method is to add +1 to an ability score after every 4 additional HD added. This is a change to Pathifnder from 3.5, which did indeed add that +1 every HD divisible by 4. And it's a change that several of us at Paizo were in fact slow to notice, so you do see it done both ways in our adventures.

  (Spelling "Pathifnder" as in the original.)
- **2010, the earlier post, which the 2014 one reverses.** [Advancing monsters - stat increases](https://paizo.com/threads/rzs2ke2c), 2010-01-19:
  > Technically, monsters with racial HD do not lose ability scores for HD reductions. … While they DO gain +1 to an ability score each time they gain enough HD to hit a number divisible by 4 (not by every 4 HD above their starting HD), they do not lose a +1 to an ability score for lowering their HD.

**Neither post is an official ruling.** Taken at face value, the 2014 reading counts every HD added to the base creature, whether racial or class, and ignores the creature's original racial HD. For a Monster PC whose racial HD are fixed by the race, that works out the same as counting class levels.

## What this means for the data model

- **Feats, skill-rank cap, BAB:** compute from **total Hit Dice** (racial + class), per source for BAB. Official text and FAQ back this.
- **Base saves:** sum per source, racial progression plus each class progression. This is official by example only (Monster Codex), with no explicit text.
- **Character level:** keep it as **class levels only**, which matches the CRB definition. Racial HD satisfying "character level N" prerequisites, or feeding "1/2 your character level" DCs, has no official support either way. Leave it to the advisory "accept either" warning (#215).
- **Ability increases:** no official answer. The only staff word (2014) points to "every 4 HD added beyond the base creature". For a Monster PC whose racial HD are fixed, that equals counting class levels. Keep this as an "accept either" warning too.
