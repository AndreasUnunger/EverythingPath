## 1. General stacking rules

**Bonus (CRB Common Terms):** "Bonuses are numerical values that are added to checks and statistical scores. Most bonuses have a type, and as a general rule, bonuses of the same type are not cumulative (do not "stack")—only the greater bonus granted applies."

**Stacking (CRB Common Terms):** "Stacking refers to the act of adding together bonuses or penalties that apply to one particular check or statistic. Generally speaking, most bonuses of the same type do not stack. Instead, only the highest bonus applies. Most penalties do stack, meaning that their values are added together. Penalties and bonuses generally stack with one another, meaning that the penalties might negate or exceed part or all of the bonuses, and vice versa."

**Bonus Types (CRB Bonus Types, p. 208):** "Usually, a bonus has a type that indicates how the spell grants the bonus. The important aspect of bonus types is that two bonuses of the same type don't generally stack. With the exception of dodge bonuses, most circumstance bonuses, and racial bonuses, only the better bonus of a given type works (see Combining Magical Effects). The same principle applies to penalties—a character taking two or more penalties of the same type applies only the worst one, although most penalties have no type and thus always stack. Bonuses without a type always stack, unless they are from the same source."

**Combining Magic Effects (CRB p. 208):**

- "Stacking Effects: Spells that provide bonuses or penalties on attack rolls, damage rolls, saving throws, and other attributes usually do not stack with themselves. More generally, two bonuses of the same type don't stack even if they come from different spells (or from effects other than spells; see Bonus Types, above)."
- "Different Bonus Types: The bonuses or penalties from two different spells stack if the modifiers are of different types. A bonus that doesn't have a type stacks with any bonus."
- "Same Effect More than Once in Different Strengths: In cases when two or more identical spells are operating in the same area or on the same target, but at different strengths, only the one with the highest strength applies."
- "Same Effect with Differing Results: The same spell can sometimes produce varying effects if applied to the same recipient more than once. Usually the last spell in the series trumps the others. None of the previous spells are actually removed or dispelled, but their effects become irrelevant while the final spell in the series lasts."
- "Instantaneous Effects: Two or more spells with instantaneous durations work cumulatively when they affect the same target."

**Multiplying (CRB Common Terms):** "When you are asked to apply more than one multiplier to a roll, the multipliers are not multiplied by one another. Instead, you combine them into a single multiplier, with each extra multiple adding 1 less than its value to the first multiple. For example, if you are asked to apply a ×2 multiplier twice, the result would be ×3, not ×4."

**Rounding (CRB Common Terms):** "Unless otherwise stated, always round down."

Two other CRB modifiers carry a name but are not stated to be bonus types:

- Flanking: "you get a +2 flanking bonus" (CRB Combat, Flanking). Whether "flanking" is a type: **open: rules silent/ambiguous.**
- Cover: "the target has cover (+4 to AC)" and "Cover grants you a +2 bonus on Reflex saves" (CRB Cover). Incorporeal touch attacks "ignore cover bonuses" (CRB Armor Class). Whether "cover" is a type: **open: rules silent/ambiguous.**

Two CRB items grant an untyped AC bonus that stacks explicitly:

- Defending weapon: "transfer some or all of the weapon's enhancement bonus to his AC as a bonus that stacks with all others" (CRB Magic Weapons).
- Class skill: "You gain a +3 bonus on all class skills that you put ranks into. If you have more than one class and both grant you a class skill bonus, these bonuses do not stack." (CRB Acquiring Skills)

### 2.3 Enhancement bonuses go on a specific thing

The CRB tracks enhancement bonuses per item or per statistic. Enhancement bonuses on different targets stack with each other.

- Armor: "Enhancement Bonuses: Enhancement bonuses apply to your armor to increase the armor bonus it provides." (CRB Armor Class)
- Armor and shield: "Magic armor bonuses are enhancement bonuses, never rise above +5, and stack with regular armor bonuses (and with shield and magic shield enhancement bonuses)." "Shields: Shield enhancement bonuses stack with armor enhancement bonuses." (CRB Magic Armor)
- Natural armor: barkskin "grants a +2 enhancement bonus to the creature's existing natural armor bonus." "The enhancement bonus provided by barkskin stacks with the target's natural armor bonus, but not with other enhancement bonuses to natural armor. A creature without natural armor has an effective natural armor bonus of +0." (CRB spell barkskin). Amulet of natural armor: "an enhancement bonus to his natural armor from +1 to +5" (CRB Wondrous Items).
- Weapons: "Wielding it provides a +1 enhancement bonus on attack rolls." "The enhancement bonus granted by the masterwork quality doesn't stack with the enhancement bonus provided by the weapon's magic." (CRB Equipment, Masterwork Weapons). "The enhancement bonus from a ranged weapon does not stack with the enhancement bonus from ammunition. Only the higher of the two enhancement bonuses applies." (CRB Magic Weapons)
- Ability scores: bull's strength "+4 enhancement bonus to Strength"; belt of giant strength "an enhancement bonus to Strength of +2, +4, or +6".
- Speed: haste's speed increase "counts as an enhancement bonus" (CRB spell haste).
- Shield bash: "An enhancement bonus on a shield does not improve the effectiveness of a shield bash made with it" (CRB Equipment).

## 3. Penalties

**The two CRB statements conflict:**

- CRB Common Terms: "Penalties do not have a type and most penalties stack with one another."
- CRB Bonus Types (p. 208): "The same principle applies to penalties—a character taking two or more penalties of the same type applies only the worst one, although most penalties have no type and thus always stack."

**Typed penalties exist in the CRB**, so "Penalties do not have a type" is not true without exception:

- enlarge person: "a –2 size penalty to Dexterity"; reduce person: "a –2 size penalty to Strength" (CRB spells).
- belt of dwarvenkind: "a –2 competence penalty on similar checks when dealing with anyone else" (CRB Wondrous Items).

**Penalties that the CRB says explicitly stack or do not stack:**

- Armor and shield check penalties: "If a character is wearing armor and using a shield, both armor check penalties apply." (CRB Armor)
- Nonproficiency: "The penalty for nonproficiency with armor stacks with the penalty for shields." (CRB Armor)
- Armor against load: "If your character is wearing armor, use the worse figure (from armor or from load) for each category. Do not stack the penalties." (CRB Carrying Capacity)
- Buckler: "you take a –1 penalty on attack rolls while doing so. This penalty stacks with those that may apply for fighting with your off hand and for fighting with two weapons." (CRB Equipment)
- AC penalties carry to CMD: "Any penalties to a creature's AC also apply to its CMD." (CRB Combat Maneuvers)

**Same-source penalties:** the CRB gives a same-source exception only for bonuses ("Bonuses without a type always stack, unless they are from the same source"). For penalties, the nearest text is "Spells that provide bonuses or penalties on attack rolls, damage rolls, saving throws, and other attributes usually do not stack with themselves" (CRB Combining Magic Effects). **Open: rules silent/ambiguous** on whether untyped penalties from the same source stack in general.

**Open: rules silent/ambiguous:** which penalties are "most" in "most penalties stack". The CRB never lists the ones that do not.

## 4. The same-source rule

**Rule:** "Bonuses without a type always stack, unless they are from the same source." (CRB Bonus Types). The FAQ restates the principle: "Generally, effects do not stack if they are from the same source (Core Rulebook page 208, Combining Magical Effects)." (FAQ CRB, Temporary Hit Points, [#v5748eaic9r43](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9r43))

**What counts as "the same source":** the CRB never defines "source". **Open: rules silent/ambiguous** in general. The official text covers these cases:

| Case | Ruling | Official wording |
|---|---|---|
| Same spell twice | Does not stack; the strongest applies, or the last one if the results differ | "usually do not stack with themselves"; "only the one with the highest strength applies"; "Usually the last spell in the series trumps the others." (CRB Combining Magic Effects) |
| Same spell, one specific case | Multiple hastes do not stack | "Multiple haste effects don't stack." (CRB spell haste) |
| Same ability modifier added twice | Does not stack; the ability bonus is the source | "No. An ability bonus, such as "Strength bonus", is considered to be the same source for the purpose of bonuses from the same source not stacking. However, you can still add, for instance "a deflection bonus equal to your Charisma modifier" and your Charisma modifier. For this purpose, however, the paladin's untyped "bonus equal to her Charisma bonus (if any) on all saving throws" from divine grace is considered to be the same as "Charisma bonus (if any)", and the same would be true for any other untyped "bonus equal to her [ability score] bonus" constructions." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9sgk](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9sgk)) |
| Temporary hit points from the same source | Do not stack; different sources do | "Although temporary hit points are not a "bonus," the principle still applies." "Temporary hit points from different sources (such as an aid spell, a use of energy drain, and a vampiric touch spell) still stack with each other." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9r43](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9r43)) |
| Same feat taken again | Does not stack | Skill Focus: "You can gain this feat multiple times. Its effects do not stack. Each time you take the feat, it applies to a new skill." (CRB Feats) |
| Same class feature from two classes | Does not stack unless the ability says so | "No—unless an ability specifically says it stacks with similar abilities (such as an assassin's sneak attack), or adds in some way based on the character's total class levels (such as improved uncanny dodge), the abilities don't stack and you have to use them separately." (FAQ CRB, Channel Energy, [#v5748eaic9o80](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9o80)) |
| Class skill bonus from two classes | Does not stack | "these bonuses do not stack" (CRB Acquiring Skills) |
| Several size changes | Do not stack; the largest applies | "As per the rules on size changes, size changes do not stack, so if you have multiple size changing effects ... only the largest applies. The same is true of effective size increases ... However, you can have one of each and they do work together" (FAQ CRB, Size increases, [#v5748eaic9t5u](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9t5u)) |
| "Does not stack with" on non-bonus effects | Only effects on the same rules component are suppressed | "that means that the effects that apply to the same rules component or situation do not stack, so if they apply different non-bonus effects to the same rules component, the most recent spell takes precedent." (FAQ APG [#v5748eaic9u3g](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fn#v5748eaic9u3g)) |
| Dodge bonus from the same source | **open: rules silent/ambiguous** | The FAQ calls this "a strange place in the rules where bonuses don't stack from the same source but dodge bonuses always stack" and says only that Paizo was "leaning toward" non-stacking for one talent (FAQ APG, offensive defense, [#v5748eaic9p1m](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fn#v5748eaic9p1m)). |
| Two copies of the same item | **open: rules silent/ambiguous** | No CRB or FAQ text was found. The nearest text is the general rule "unless they are from the same source". |
| Circumstance bonuses that do not stack | **open: rules silent/ambiguous** | Only "most circumstance bonuses" stack (CRB Bonus Types). The CRB does not list the exceptions. |

## 5. Where each bonus type may apply

**Open: rules silent/ambiguous.** The Core Rulebook has no general rule that limits a bonus type to particular statistics. The nearest CRB text: "Usually, a bonus has a type that indicates how the spell grants the bonus." (CRB Bonus Types). Ultimate Magic's design table (appendix) is the only official type-to-statistic mapping found, and it is outside the CRB.

The CRB does fix some types to particular targets:

- Armor bonus: "Each type of armor grants an armor bonus to AC" (CRB Armor). Mage armor: "+4 armor bonus to AC".
- Shield bonus: "shields grant a shield bonus to AC" (CRB Armor).
- Natural armor: "you receive a bonus to your AC" (CRB Armor Class).
- Enhancement to armor or shields raises the armor or shield bonus, not the wearer (section 2.3).
- Deflection: "Magical deflection effects ward off attacks and improve your AC." (CRB Armor Class)
- Dodge is **not** limited to AC in the CRB: haste grants "a +1 dodge bonus to AC and Reflex saves".

Targets the CRB applies each type to in its own examples (these are observations, not a restriction rule):

| Type | Targets seen in CRB text |
|---|---|
| Armor, shield, natural armor, deflection | AC |
| Dodge | AC, Reflex saves (haste) |
| Enhancement | armor bonus, shield bonus, natural armor bonus, weapon attack and damage, ability scores, speed |
| Inherent | ability scores |
| Size | Strength, Dexterity (enlarge/reduce person) |
| Morale | attack rolls, saves, skill checks, Strength, Constitution, Will saves |
| Luck | attack rolls, weapon damage, saves, skill checks, ability checks |
| Competence | attack roll, saving throw, skill check (guidance); Charisma checks and skills (belt of dwarvenkind) |
| Insight | attack roll (true strike), AC (ioun stone) |
| Resistance | saves |
| Sacred, profane | channel energy DCs; attack, damage, saves (desecrate) |
| Alchemical | Fortitude saves vs. poison |
| Racial | saves, skill checks, CMD, caster level checks (elf) |
| Circumstance | grapple checks |

## 6. AC, touch AC, flat-footed AC, CMB and CMD

### 6.1 Armor Class

"Your AC is equal to the following: 10 + armor bonus + shield bonus + Dexterity modifier + other modifiers" (CRB Armor Class). The other modifiers named there: enhancement (to armor), deflection, natural armor, dodge, size modifier.

Size: "You receive a bonus or penalty to your AC based on your size." Colossal –8, Gargantuan –4, Huge –2, Large –1, Medium +0, Small +1, Tiny +2, Diminutive +4, Fine +8 (CRB Armor Class, Table 8-1).

### 6.2 Dexterity and the max Dex cap

- "Maximum Dex Bonus: This number is the maximum Dexterity bonus to AC that this type of armor allows. Dexterity bonuses in excess of this number are reduced to this number for the purposes of determining the wearer's AC. ... This restriction doesn't affect any other Dexterity-related abilities." (CRB Armor)
- "Even if a character's Dexterity bonus to AC drops to 0 because of armor, this situation does not count as losing his Dexterity bonus to AC." (CRB Armor)
- "A character's encumbrance (the amount of gear carried, including armor) may also restrict the maximum Dexterity bonus that can be applied to his Armor Class." "Shields: Shields do not affect a character's maximum Dexterity bonus, except for tower shields." (CRB Armor)
- Armor against load: "use the worse figure (from armor or from load) for each category. Do not stack the penalties." (CRB Carrying Capacity)
- "Sometimes you can't use your Dexterity bonus (if you have one). If you can't react to a blow, you can't use your Dexterity bonus to AC. If you don't have a Dexterity bonus, your AC does not change." (CRB Armor Class)
- Dodge is tied to Dex: "Any situation that denies you your Dexterity bonus also denies you dodge bonuses. (Wearing armor, however, does not limit these bonuses the way it limits a Dexterity bonus to AC.)" (CRB Armor Class)

### 6.3 Touch AC

"When you are the target of a touch attack, your AC doesn't include any armor bonus, shield bonus, or natural armor bonus. All other modifiers, such as your size modifier, Dexterity modifier, and deflection bonus (if any) apply normally." (CRB Armor Class)

Incorporeal touch: "Incorporeal touch attacks work similarly to normal touch attacks except that they also ignore cover bonuses. Incorporeal touch attacks do not ignore armor bonuses granted by force effects, such as mage armor and bracers of armor." The shield spell's bonus "applies against incorporeal touch attacks, since it is a force effect." (CRB Armor Class; CRB spell shield)

Enhancement bonuses to armor, shield or natural armor raise those bonuses (section 2.3), so touch AC excludes them along with the bonus they raise.

FAQ: the duelist's Int bonus to AC "applies to the character's touch AC" (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9qdi](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9qdi)).

### 6.4 Flat-footed AC

- "At the start of a battle, before you have had a chance to act (specifically, before your first regular turn in the initiative order), you are flat-footed. You can't use your Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) while flat-footed." (CRB Initiative)
- Condition: "A flat-footed character loses his Dexterity bonus to AC (if any) and cannot make attacks of opportunity." (CRB Conditions)
- Dodge bonuses are lost too, by the CRB Armor Class rule quoted in 6.2.
- Every other type still applies. The CRB names no other type that is lost.

Errata (FAQ CRB, Grapple, [#v5748eaic9og8](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9og8)): "1) Grappling does not deny you your Dex bonus to AC, whether you are the grappler or the target." "3) Being pinned does not make you flat-footed, but you are denied your Dex bonus." The entry gives the matching changes to Table 8-6 and to the Pinned condition ("change "flat-footed" to "denied its Dexterity bonus.""). So being denied the Dex bonus and being flat-footed are separate states.

Helpless: "a helpless character is treated as having a Dexterity of 0, giving him a –5 penalty to AC against both melee and ranged attacks (for a total of –9 against melee and –5 against ranged). A helpless character is also flat-footed." (CRB Helpless Defenders)

### 6.5 CMB

"CMB = Base attack bonus + Strength modifier + special size modifier" "Creatures that are size Tiny or smaller use their Dexterity modifier in place of their Strength modifier to determine their CMB." Special size modifier: "Fine –8, Diminutive –4, Tiny –2, Small –1, Medium +0, Large +1, Huge +2, Gargantuan +4, Colossal +8". "Add any bonuses you currently have on attack rolls due to spells, feats, and other effects. These bonuses must be applicable to the weapon or attack used to perform the maneuver." (CRB Combat Maneuvers)

FAQ: with Weapon Finesse, "if you're attempting a disarm, sunder, or trip maneuver, you can apply your Dex bonus instead of your Str mod on the combat maneuver check ... For other combat maneuvers, you use the normal rule for determining CMB (Str instead of Dex)." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9ojt](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9ojt))

### 6.6 CMD

"CMD = 10 + Base attack bonus + Strength modifier + Dexterity modifier + special size modifier" ... "A creature can also add any circumstance, deflection, dodge, insight, luck, morale, profane, and sacred bonuses to AC to its CMD. Any penalties to a creature's AC also apply to its CMD. A flat-footed creature does not add its Dexterity bonus to its CMD." (CRB Combat Maneuvers)

FAQ: "As of the 6th printing of the Core Rulebook, the canny defense ability grants a dodge bonus to AC, which does improve the duelist's CMD. (Previous to the 6th printing, the canny defense bonus was not one of the types of bonuses listed in the CMD rules, and therefore did not add to CMD.)" (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9qfa](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9qfa)). This confirms the CMD list is closed: AC bonuses of types not listed do not reach CMD.

### 6.7 Fan-out by bonus type

Yes means the CRB text quoted above says it applies. Not listed means the CMD rule does not name the type.

### 6.8 Open items for AC and CMD

- **Max Dex cap on CMD. Open: rules silent/ambiguous.** The CMD formula adds the "Dexterity modifier" and says nothing about armor. The nearest text, about Max Dex: "This restriction doesn't affect any other Dexterity-related abilities." (CRB Armor)
- **Dodge bonuses to CMD when flat-footed. Open: rules silent/ambiguous.** The CMD rule only says "A flat-footed creature does not add its Dexterity bonus to its CMD." The nearest text is the AC rule: "Any situation that denies you your Dexterity bonus also denies you dodge bonuses." (CRB Armor Class)
- **AC size modifier in CMD. Open: rules silent/ambiguous.** CMD has its own "special size modifier" (Large +1). The AC size modifier for Large is "–1", and "Any penalties to a creature's AC also apply to its CMD." The CRB does not say whether that AC size penalty is meant to also reach CMD.
- **Untyped AC bonuses and CMD. Open: rules silent/ambiguous.** The CMD list names types only. The defending property's "bonus that stacks with all others" and cover's "+4 to AC" are not mentioned.

## 7. Other modifier targets

- **Attack (CRB Attack Bonus):** "Your attack bonus with a melee weapon is the following: Base attack bonus + Strength modifier + size modifier" and "With a ranged weapon, your attack bonus is the following: Base attack bonus + Dexterity modifier + size modifier + range penalty"
- **BAB (CRB Common Terms):** "Each creature has a base attack bonus and it represents its skill in combat. As a character gains levels or Hit Dice, his base attack bonus improves." BAB is not a bonus type. Whether it can be the target of typed bonuses: **open: rules silent/ambiguous**.
- **Saves (CRB Saving Throws):** "Your saving throw modifier is: Base save bonus + ability modifier". Fortitude uses Constitution, Reflex uses Dexterity, Will uses Wisdom.
- **Initiative (CRB Initiative):** "An initiative check is a Dexterity check. Each character applies his or her Dexterity modifier to the roll, as well as other modifiers from feats, spells, and other effects." FAQ: "when adding both Wisdom and Dexterity on initiative checks, initiative is still a Dexterity check, not a Wisdom check." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9tga](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9tga))
- **Skills (CRB Acquiring Skills):** "Each skill rank grants a +1 bonus on checks made using that skill." ## 8. Ability scores

### 8.1 Modifiers

"A positive modifier is called a bonus, and a negative modifier is called a penalty." (CRB Determine Bonuses). An ability bonus is its own "source" for stacking purposes (FAQ, section 4).

### 8.2 Temporary bonuses (duration 1 day or less)

CRB Ability Score Bonuses (p. 554): "Some spells and abilities increase your ability scores. Ability score increases with a duration of 1 day or less give only temporary bonuses. For every two points of increase to a single ability, apply a +1 bonus to the skills and statistics listed with the relevant ability."

The CRB then lists per ability. Key points:

- Strength: "Strength-based skill checks, melee attack rolls, and weapon damage rolls (if they rely on Strength). The bonus also applies to your Combat Maneuver Bonus (if you are Small or larger) and to your Combat Maneuver Defense."
- Dexterity: "Dexterity-based skill checks, ranged attack rolls, initiative checks, and Reflex saving throws. The bonus also applies to your Armor Class, your Combat Maneuver Bonus (if you are Tiny or smaller), and your Combat Maneuver Defense."
- Constitution: "a bonus on your Fortitude saving throws. In addition, multiply your total Hit Dice by this bonus and add that amount to your current and total hit points. When the bonus ends, remove this total from your current and total hit points."
- Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma: the related skill checks, Will saves (Wisdom), "any spell DCs based on" that ability, and for Charisma "the DC to resist your channeled energy".

Hit points from temporary Con are **not** temporary hit points: "Hit points gained by a temporary increase in Constitution score are not temporary hit points. They go away when the subject's Constitution drops back to normal. They are not lost first as temporary hit points are." (CRB spell bearSEndurance)

Temporary bonuses give no bonus spells and no skill ranks: fox's cunning: "Wizards (and other spellcasters who rely on Intelligence) affected by this spell do not gain any additional bonus spells for the increased Intelligence, but the save DCs for spells they cast while under this spell's effect do increase. This spell doesn't grant extra skill ranks." Owl's wisdom: "Clerics, druids, and rangers (and other Wisdom-based spellcasters) who receive owl's wisdom do not gain any additional bonus spells for the increased Wisdom, but the save DCs for their spells increase." (CRB spells foxSCunning, owlSWisdom)

FAQ (changes the reading of the list above):

- "Temporary ability bonuses should apply to anything relating to that ability score, just as permanent ability score bonuses do. The section in the glossary was very tight on space and it was not possible to list every single ability score-related game effect" ... "A temporary ability score bonus should affect all of the same stats and rolls that a permanent ability score bonus does." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9rbg](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9rbg))
- "Temporary ability score increases should affect supernatural ability DCs based on those ability scores, such as a medusa's gaze attack or a witch's hexes." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9quz](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9quz))

**Open: rules silent/ambiguous:** how the FAQ's "all of the same stats and rolls" squares with fox's cunning and owl's wisdom denying bonus spells and skill ranks. The spell texts are specific; the FAQ does not mention them.

### 8.3 Permanent bonuses (the 24-hour rule)

"Permanent Bonuses: Ability bonuses with a duration greater than 1 day actually increase the relevant ability score after 24 hours. Modify all skills and statistics related to that ability. This might cause you to gain skill points, hit points, and other bonuses. These bonuses should be noted separately in case they are removed." (CRB Ability Score Bonuses)

Items: belt of giant strength: "Treat this as a temporary ability bonus for the first 24 hours the belt is worn." All ten ability-enhancing belts and headbands in the CRB use this sentence. (CRB Wondrous Items)

Int items and skill ranks: headband of vast intelligence "has one skill associated with it per +2 bonus it grants. After being worn for 24 hours, the headband grants a number of skill ranks in those skills equal to the wearer's total Hit Dice. These ranks do not stack with the ranks a creature already possesses." (CRB Wondrous Items). FAQ: "No. The skill associated with the magic item represents the "retroactive" skill ranks you'd get from the item increasing your Intelligence. You don't get the item's built-in skill ranks and another set to assign however you want." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9og7](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9og7)). Languages: "Technically, Int-enhancing items such as a headband of vast intelligence should grant a specific language (in the same way they do for skill ranks)." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9qos](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9qos))

Level-up increases: "At 4th level, a character can increase one ability score by +1. This is a typeless, nonmagical bonus that cannot be changed once selected." ... "stacks with all other bonuses." (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9nd2](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9nd2))

Enhancement and inherent sizes: "officially the game should only have even-numbered enhancement bonuses to ability scores. If you want an odd-numbered ability score bonus, you'll need to pay for an inherent bonus" (FAQ CRB [#v5748eaic9qb8](https://paizo.com/paizo/faq/v5748nruor1fm#v5748eaic9qb8))

**Open: rules silent/ambiguous:** the effect of the 24-hour rule on saves. The CRB says "Modify all skills and statistics related to that ability"; saves are not named separately. The issue's question about saves is answered only by that general sentence.

### 8.4 Ability damage, ability penalty, ability drain

All quotes from CRB Ability Damage/Drain (p. 555).

| | Changes the score? | Effect | Floor / death | Healing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Damage | No: "This damage does not actually reduce an ability, but it does apply a penalty to the skills and statistics that are based on that ability." | "For every 2 points of damage you take to a single ability, apply a –1 penalty to skills and statistics listed with the relevant ability." Con: "multiply your total Hit Dice by this penalty and subtract that amount from your current and total hit points. Lost hit points are restored when the damage to your Constitution is healed." | "If the amount of ability damage you have taken equals or exceeds your ability score, you immediately fall unconscious until the damage is less than your ability score. ## 9. FAQ and errata that change the above

- Ability modifier counts as one source and does not stack with itself (section 4).
- Temporary hit points from the same source do not stack (section 4).
- Temporary ability bonuses affect everything a permanent bonus would, including supernatural ability DCs (section 8.2).
- Level-up ability increases are typeless and stack with all other bonuses (section 8.3).
- Headband skill ranks are the retroactive Int ranks, not extra ones (section 8.3).
- Size changes do not stack; actual and effective size changes combine (section 4).
- Canny defense became a dodge bonus in the 6th printing and so reaches CMD (section 6.6).
- Grappling does not deny Dex; pinned denies Dex but is not flat-footed; Table 8-6 and the Pinned condition were corrected (section 6.4).
- Weapon Finesse applies to CMB only for disarm, sunder and trip (section 6.5).
- "Does not stack with" on non-bonus effects suppresses only the overlapping rules component (section 4).

## 10. Comparison with the existing corpus (`research/pf1-core-rules`)

Checked `docs/ai/pf1-core-rules/pf1-rules.md`, `pf1-tables.md` and `README.md` on branch `research/pf1-core-rules` (commit 462de42).

**Contradicted by official text:**

1. `pf1-rules.md`, Ability Modifiers: "a Con increase adds (total Hit Dice x bonus) temporary hp." The official text says the opposite: "Hit points gained by a temporary increase in Constitution score are not temporary hit points. ... They are not lost first as temporary hit points are." (CRB spell bearSEndurance). The glossary says to add them "to your current and total hit points".
2. `pf1-rules.md`, Bonus Types and Stacking, and `README.md`: penalty stacking is described as a conflict with a "Builder rule of thumb: treat untyped penalties as stacking; if a typed penalty is ever modelled, apply only the worst of a given type." The rule of thumb is not official text. The corpus also implies typed penalties may never occur ("if a typed penalty is ever modelled"); the CRB has them (enlarge person "–2 size penalty to Dexterity", belt of dwarvenkind "–2 competence penalty").
3. `pf1-rules.md`, Ability Modifiers: `A positive modifier is a "bonus", a negative one a "penalty" (it behaves as a typeless value, see stacking).` The CRB only says "A positive modifier is called a bonus, and a negative modifier is called a penalty." The FAQ adds a rule the corpus leaves out: the same ability bonus counts as one source and does not stack with itself (FAQ CRB #v5748eaic9sgk). The parenthesis "behaves as a typeless value" is not official text.

**Not supported, or missing a needed qualifier:**

4. `pf1-rules.md`, Ability Modifiers: "temporary increases (duration <= 1 day) give a temporary bonus per 2 points to the related skills and stats". Correct, but the corpus does not have the FAQ ruling that temporary bonuses "should affect all of the same stats and rolls that a permanent ability score bonus does", nor the spell texts that deny bonus spells and skill ranks.
5. `pf1-rules.md`, Ability Modifiers: "Permanent increases (e.g. level-up, inherent bonuses) change the score". The CRB's permanent rule is about "Ability bonuses with a duration greater than 1 day", which "actually increase the relevant ability score after 24 hours". Level-up increases are covered by a separate FAQ ("typeless, nonmagical bonus"), not by the 24-hour rule. The 24-hour delay itself is missing from the corpus.
6. `pf1-rules.md`, Skills: "Retroactive Int changes: Int changes change ranks". Official text: "This might cause you to gain skill points" (CRB) and, for headbands, the item's listed skills are the retroactive ranks (FAQ CRB #v5748eaic9og7). The corpus does not have the headband limit.
7. `pf1-rules.md`, Bonus Types and Stacking: "Builder implementation should keep the type per modifier and make the stacking exception an attribute of the type (dodge, racial, circumstance = stack)". Circumstance is "most circumstance bonuses", not all; the exceptions are open (section 4).
8. `pf1-rules.md`, Bonus Types and Stacking: "Enhancement bonuses on armor add to the armor bonus of that armor." Correct, but incomplete for stacking: shield enhancement stacks with armor enhancement, and barkskin's enhancement to natural armor is a separate stack (section 2.3). A single "enhancement" bucket would be wrong.
9. `pf1-tables.md`, Bonus Types table: "Size | No". The type stacking is consistent with official text, but size also exists as a typed penalty, and the FAQ separates actual and effective size changes (section 4).
10. `pf1-rules.md`, Combat Maneuver Bonus and Defense: the corpus states the CMD rule correctly. It does not mention the open questions on max Dex, dodge when flat-footed, and the AC size penalty (section 6.8).
11. `README.md` out-of-scope list includes "ability damage/drain". Section 8.4 now covers it.

**Supported by official text:** the general typed/untyped stacking rules, the dodge/circumstance/racial exceptions, same-effect rules, the multiplier rule, armor and shield bonus non-stacking, touch and incorporeal touch composition, flat-footed losing Dex and dodge, the CMD type list, the class-skill +3 non-stacking, armor check penalty rules, and "use the worse figure" for armor against load.
